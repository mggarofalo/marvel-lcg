# PLAY, PUT INTO PLAY

Playing a card involves paying the card’s cost and placing the card in the play area. This causes the card to enter play *(or, in the case of an event card, to resolve its ability and be placed in the discard pile)*. Cards are played from a player’s hand. Some abilities cause cards to be put into play. This bypasses the need to pay the card’s cost as well as any restrictions or prohibitions regarding playing that card. A card that is put into play enters play in its controller’s play area.

<a id="play-put-into-play-1"></a>
1. Cards with the text “[type] form only” can only be played or put into play by a player whose identity is in the specified form.

<a id="play-put-into-play-2"></a>
2. When an event card is played, place it on the table, resolve its ability, and place the card in its owner’s discard pile.

<a id="play-put-into-play-3"></a>
3. A card that is put into play is not considered to have been played.

<a id="play-put-into-play-4"></a>
4. When a card is put into play, its resource cost is ignored.

<a id="play-put-into-play-5"></a>
5. Unless otherwise stated by the “put into play” effect, cards that are put into play must do so in a play area or state that matches the rules of playing the card.
