# DRAW, DRAWING CARDS

If a player is instructed to draw one or more cards, those cards are drawn from the top of their deck one at a time.

<a id="draw-drawing-cards-1"></a>
1. Drawn cards are added to a player’s hand.

<a id="draw-drawing-cards-2"></a>
2. Each player’s hand size is checked at the end of the player phase. If a player has more cards in their hand than their hand size value at this time, they must choose and discard cards from hand until they are at their hand size.
