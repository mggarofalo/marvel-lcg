# ENERGY RESOURCE

An energy resource ([energy]) is one of the four types of resources

<a id="energy-resource-1"></a>
1. Energy resources can be spent to pay the resource cost of cards and abilities.

<a id="energy-resource-2"></a>
2. Some card abilities specifically require energy resources to be spent in order to resolve their effects
