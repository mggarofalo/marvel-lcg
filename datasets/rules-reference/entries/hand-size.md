# HAND SIZE

Each player checks their hand size at the end of the player phase, either discarding down to or drawing up to the number of cards indicated by their hand size value.

<a id="hand-size-1"></a>
1. When drawing up to their hand size, a player draws cards one at a time, checking after each card is drawn whether they are at their hand size.
