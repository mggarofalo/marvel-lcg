# CANCEL

Some card abilities can cancel card or game effects.

<a id="cancel-1"></a>
1. Cancel abilities interrupt the initiation of effects and prevent them from resolving.

<a id="cancel-2"></a>
2. Anytime the effects of an ability are canceled, the ability *(apart from its effects)* is still regarded as initiated, and any costs are still paid. Only the effects are prevented from initiating, and do not resolve.

<a id="cancel-3"></a>
3. If the effects of an event card are canceled, the card is still considered played, and it is discarded.

<a id="cancel-4"></a>
4. If the effects of a treachery card are canceled, the card is still regarded as revealed, and it is still placed in the encounter discard pile.

<a id="cancel-5"></a>
5. Cancel effects are considered a subtype of replacement effect, with the canceled effect being replaced with no effect.
    <a id="cancel-5-1"></a>
    - Abilities dependent on the canceled effect cannot trigger as the canceled effect is not considered to have occurred.
