# TUCK

When a player is instructed to tuck a card under another card, they place the tucked card faceup under the other card.

<a id="tuck-1"></a>
1. Tucked cards are not in play and are not considered “attached” to the card under which they are tucked.

<a id="tuck-2"></a>
2. When a card leaves play, each card tucked under it is discarded.
