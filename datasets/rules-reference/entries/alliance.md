# ALLIANCE

When a player declares their intention to play a card with the alliance keyword, any player(s) may help pay the costs for that card.

<a id="alliance-1"></a>
1. The alliance keyword is equivalent to the following constant ability: “While paying costs for this card, any player may contribute to paying those costs.”

<a id="alliance-2"></a>
2. Only the player playing the card with the alliance keyword is considered to be resolving that card.
