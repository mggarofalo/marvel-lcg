# REFERENTIAL ABILITY

Some abilities refer to specific cards by name. These are called referential abilities. If an ability refers to a title shared by multiple cards in the game, that ability refers only to the card(s) that match the criteria highest in this list:

<a id="referential-ability-step-1"></a>
1. The card on which the referential ability is printed. Such an ability is a self-referential ability. *For example, the basic ally Spider-Man’s ability reads: “***Response***: After Spider-Man attacks or thwarts, choose another character ready that character.” This ability refers to the card it is printed on and does not trigger when another card called “Spider-Man” attacks or thwarts.*

<a id="referential-ability-step-2"></a>
2. Cards associated with the same identity, including: The identity card Identity-specific cards The identity’s obligation cards The identity’s nemesis set Cards belonging to a side deck used by the identity.

<a id="referential-ability-step-3"></a>
3. Player cards (if the ability is on a player card) or encounter cards (if the ability is on an encounter card).
