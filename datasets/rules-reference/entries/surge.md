# SURGE

When an encounter card with this keyword is revealed, the player resolving the card deals themself a facedown encounter card from the top of the encounter deck.

<a id="surge-1"></a>
1. The surge keyword is equivalent to the following triggered ability: “**When Revealed**: Deal yourself 1 facedown encounter card.”

<a id="surge-2"></a>
2. Complete the process of resolving the original card, as well as any response abilities that are triggered by that card being revealed, before revealing the additional card.
