# MENTAL RESOURCE

A mental resource ([mental]) is one of the four types of resources.

<a id="mental-resource-1"></a>
1. Mental resources can be spent to pay the resource cost of cards and abilities.

<a id="mental-resource-2"></a>
2. Some card abilities specifically require mental resources to be spent in order to resolve their effects.
