# IDENTITY

Identity is a player card type that represents which character a player is playing in the game. A player’s identity card is a double-sided card that represents their hero on one side and their alter-ego on the other. The side that is face up indicates the form *(hero or alter-ego)* that player is currently in.

<a id="identity-1"></a>
1. Each player begins the game in alter-ego form.

<a id="identity-2"></a>
2. If a card refers to a hero or alter-ego by title, it refers only to the identity with that title, and not to the other side of the card.

<a id="identity-3"></a>
3. Identity cards cannot be discarded from play.

<a id="identity-4"></a>
4. The faceup side of an identity card is considered to be in play. The facedown side of an identity card is considered to be out of play.
