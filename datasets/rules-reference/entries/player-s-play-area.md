# PLAYER’S PLAY AREA

A player’s play area *(also sometimes referred to as a “player’s game area”)* is the area of play where a player’s identity card, deck, hand, discard pile, and hit point dial are located. Each player in the game has their own play area.

<a id="player-s-play-area-1"></a>
1. Ally cards, support cards, and upgrade cards are placed in a player’s play area when they enter play.

<a id="player-s-play-area-2"></a>
2. Attachment cards attached to cards in the villain’s play area are not in the player’s play area.

<a id="player-s-play-area-3"></a>
3. Minion cards engaged with a player are in that player’s play area and not in the villain’s play area.

<a id="player-s-play-area-4"></a>
4. Obligation cards given to a player are in that player’s play area and not in the villain’s play area.

<a id="player-s-play-area-5"></a>
5. While card abilities can affect cards across play areas, unless a game rule or card ability states otherwise, cards cannot be played into another player’s play area.

<a id="player-s-play-area-6"></a>
6. If a player is eliminated from the game *(through their identity being defeated, or otherwise)*, their play area is removed from the game.
