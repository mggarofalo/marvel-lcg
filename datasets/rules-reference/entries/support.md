# SUPPORT

Support is a player card type that represent locations, backline supporters or friends, and other behind-the-scenes elements a hero or alter-ego might have at their disposal.

<a id="support-1"></a>
1. Support cards enter play in the back row of a player’s play area.

<a id="support-2"></a>
2. A support card is active while it is in play, and it remains in play until a card ability causes it to leave play.

<a id="support-3"></a>
3. Attacks, thwarts, defenses, action abilities, and triggered abilities that resolve from supports in play under a player’s control are not considered to be performed by that player’s identity.
