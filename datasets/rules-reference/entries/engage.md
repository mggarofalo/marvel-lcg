# ENGAGE

When a minion enters play in a player’s play area, it engages that player. Unless otherwise specified by the minion or by the effect that put the minion into play, the minion engages the player who is resolving the current encounter card.

<a id="engage-1"></a>
1. An engaged minion remains engaged with the same player until it is defeated, removed from play, or a card ability causes it to engage another player.

<a id="engage-2"></a>
2. If a card ability instructs a player to engage a minion, that minion is also considered to have engaged that player.

<a id="engage-3"></a>
3. While a minion is engaged with a player, card abilities cannot cause the minion to engage with the same player again *(as the two are already engaged)*.
