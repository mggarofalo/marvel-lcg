# TRAITS

Many cards have one or more traits listed at the top of the text box and printed in bold italics.

<a id="traits-1"></a>
1. Traits have no inherent effects on the game. Instead, some card abilities reference cards that possess or lack specific traits.

<a id="traits-2"></a>
2. Traits are not considered to be part of a card’s printed text box for the purpose of card abilities.
