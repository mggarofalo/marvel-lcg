# PLAYER PHASE

During the player phase, each player *(in player order)* takes one turn. After each player has taken a turn, the players discard down or draw up to their hand size and ready each exhausted card.

<a id="player-phase-1"></a>
1. Effects that last “until the end of the [player] phase” end after players draw up to their hand size and all cards are readied. Then, effects that resolve “when/ after the player phase ends” are resolved.
