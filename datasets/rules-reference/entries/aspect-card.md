# ASPECT CARD

Cards in the “aspect” classification are any cards that belong to the Aggression, Justice, Leadership, and/or Protection aspects.

<a id="aspect-card-1"></a>
1. When building a player deck, a player must choose one of the five aspects *(Aggression, Justice, Leadership, Protection, or ‘Pool)* to use for customization. The remainder of their deck *(the portion not allocated to their identity-specific cards)* can then be customized with cards that belong to the chosen aspect.

<a id="aspect-card-2"></a>
2. A card’s aspect is designated by the aspect’s name printed at the bottom of the card in its deckbuilding classification area.
