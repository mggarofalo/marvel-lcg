# PLAYER CARD

There are seven player card types: ally cards, event cards, identity cards, player side scheme cards, resource cards, support cards, and upgrade cards.

<a id="player-card-1"></a>
1. Player cards may belong to various classifications, such as identity-specific player cards or aspect player cards.

<a id="player-card-2"></a>
2. Most player cards have a blue card back.
