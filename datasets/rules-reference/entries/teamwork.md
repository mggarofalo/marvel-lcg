# TEAMWORK ()

After a minion with teamwork enters play and engages a player, if there is at least one other minion that shares the specified trait in play, the minion that just entered play activates against the player it is engaged with.

<a id="teamwork-1"></a>
1. The teamwork () keyword is equivalent to the following triggered ability: “**Forced Response**: After this minion enters play, if there is another minion in play, this minion activates against the engaged player.”

<a id="teamwork-2"></a>
2. If a minion with the teamwork keyword is being revealed, the teamwork keyword resolves after any “**When Revealed**” abilities on that minion are resolved.
