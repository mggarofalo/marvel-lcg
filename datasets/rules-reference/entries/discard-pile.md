# DISCARD PILE

. A discard pile is an out-of-play area that contains cards that have been discarded throughout the game.

<a id="discard-pile-1"></a>
1. Each player has their own discard pile, and the encounter deck has its own discard pile.

<a id="discard-pile-2"></a>
2. Each discard pile is open information, and may be looked at by any player at any time.

<a id="discard-pile-3"></a>
3. The order of cards in a discard pile may not be changed unless a player is instructed to do so by a card ability.

<a id="discard-pile-4"></a>
4. Any ability that would shuffle a discard pile of zero cards back into a deck does not shuffle the deck.
