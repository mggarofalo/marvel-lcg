# LUKE CAGE HERO PACK

<a id="featured-keyword-piercing"></a>
## Featured Keyword: Piercing

An attack with piercing discards any tough status cards from the target of the attack before dealing damage.

<a id="featured-keyword-team-up"></a>
## Featured Keyword: Team-Up

The team-up keyword names two characters. To include a card with the team-up keyword in your deck, your chosen identity must match one of the named characters. Additionally, a card with team-up cannot be played unless both of the named characters (identity or ally) are in play.

<a id="new-keyword-prerequisite-form-or"></a>
## New Keyword: Prerequisite (form or )

In order for a player to play a card with prerequisite (form) or prerequisite (), their identity must be in the specified form (i.e. Hero or Alter-Ego form) or have the specified trait.

<a id="new-term-unpreventable-damage"></a>
## New Term: Unpreventable Damage

Unpreventable damage dealt to a character cannot be reduced or prevented (such as by a tough status card). Unpreventable damage dealt to a character with 1 or more tough status cards does not remove them.
