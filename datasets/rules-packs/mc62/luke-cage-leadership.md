# LUKE CAGE // LEADERSHIP

Luke Cage rushes fist-first into the fray, delivering a fresh Knuckle Sandwich. He calls on allies like Jessica Jones to aid him in battle, then defends them with his Metal Bracers and all the toughness he gained from the Burstein Process. He flexes his muscles as Power Man, takes out all the bad guys Cruisin’ for a Bruisin’, and becomes Harlem’s Hero! With the Leadership aspect, hire your fellow heroes like Iron Fist or Misty Knight. Use Dynamic Duo to set up an epic team- up, or create a Defensive Formation to stop your enemies dead in their tracks. Take a Stand and inspire those allies to fight for a Righteous Purpose!
