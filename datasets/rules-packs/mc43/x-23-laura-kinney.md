# X-23 // LAURA KINNEY

X-23’s power comes from her ability to ready after taking damage. She can defend villain attacks and, if she takes any damage, ready up to defend again if necessary or else attack or thwart in the player phase. In the player phase, she can take damage from X-23’s Claws to ready herself for a big attack. Her ally Honey Badger provides her even more readying to use her increased ATK value, and she can bring Honey Badger back quickly through her alter-ego ability. With the Aggression aspect, pump up your ATK with “Now I’m Mad” and use The Direct Approach to apply your increased ATK to thwarting side schemes. Get some Specialized Training to gain a powerful upgrade that can’t be gained any other way.
