# FEATURED KEYWORDS

<a id="alliance"></a>
## Alliance

When a player declares their intention to play an alliance card, any player may help pay the costs for that card.

<a id="ranged"></a>
## Ranged

An attack with ranged ignores the retaliate keyword.

<a id="victory-x"></a>
## Victory X

When a card with the victory X keyword is defeated, place it in the victory display instead of its owner’s discard pile.
