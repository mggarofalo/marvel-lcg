# PER PLAYER () COSTS

This expansion includes player cards with a per player cost, indicated by the  icon included in the card’s cost. The cost of these cards is the numeric value multiplied by the number of players who started the scenario. Example: Wade plays the event card Break Time (46), which has a cost of 3. The scenario began with three players playing, so Wade must pay nine resources to play Break Time.
