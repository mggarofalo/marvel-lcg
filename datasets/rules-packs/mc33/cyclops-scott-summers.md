# CYCLOPS // SCOTT SUMMERS

Cyclops can fire a powerful “Optic Blast” from his eyes, but his real power comes from his tactical awareness. Attach Exploit Weakness to an enemy so it takes extra damage from each attack. Play Field Commander so you can always play your upgrades before your teammate’s turn. If you run out of upgrades, change to alter-ego and use your “Constant Training” ability to search your deck for one. With the leadership aspect, use Effective Leadership to pay for an ally and boost its stats that turn. Use Danger Room to attach Danger Room Training to an ally after it enters play to boost its stats further. Use Game Time to get extra actions from that ally while it has increased THW and ATK.
