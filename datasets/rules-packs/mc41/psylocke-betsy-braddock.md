# PSYLOCKE // BETSY BRADDOCK

Psylocke is skilled with blades, whether physical or psionic. Her double-sided upgrades provide her with bonuses to her THW on one side and her ATK on the other. Her events provide different abilities based on which weapons she has faceup when the event is played, giving her tremendous flexibility. When she uses her upgrades to pay for her events (or any other cards), she can flip those upgrades to whichever side she needs. Choosing when to flip her upgrades is key to unlocking her power. The Justice cards in this pack are focused on confusing enemies and gaining benefits from doing so. Confuse enemies with Concussive Blow and Upside the Head, then deal them extra damage with Float Like a Butterfly. Directed Force increases the damage from your attacks with keywords like piercing and overkill. Use The Power of the Mind to power Telepathy when you need extra threat removal.
