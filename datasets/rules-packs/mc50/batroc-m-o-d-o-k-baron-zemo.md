# BATROC / M.O.D.O.K. / BARON ZEMO

<a id="q-am-i-considered-to-have-defeated-one-of-these-villains-if-i-reduce-their-hit-points-to-0"></a>
## Q. Am I considered to have defeated one of these villains if I reduce their hit points to 0?

A. No, the forced effects on these villains replace the defeating of the villain, so the villain has not been defeated for the purpose of other card effects.
