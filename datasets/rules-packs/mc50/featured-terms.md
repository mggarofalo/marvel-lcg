# FEATURED TERMS

<a id="find"></a>
## “Find”

When you are instructed to find a card, search each area where the card could be found (play area, set-aside area, player deck, discard pile, encounter deck, etc). Do not search the victory display, cards that have been removed from the game, or facedown encounter cards currently dealt to players. Do not search game areas unnecessarily if you know where the card you are looking for can be found.

<a id="tuck"></a>
## “Tuck”

When you are instructed to tuck a card under another card, place the tucked card faceup under the other card. The tucked card is not in play. When a card leaves play, any cards tucked under it are discarded.
