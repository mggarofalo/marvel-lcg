# MARIA HILL

<a id="q-how-many-schemes-or-enemies-can-be-chosen-as-targets-for-all-points-bulletin-3"></a>
## Q. How many schemes or enemies can be chosen as targets for All-Points Bulletin (3)?

A. You can choose a different scheme or enemy to target for each support you control.
