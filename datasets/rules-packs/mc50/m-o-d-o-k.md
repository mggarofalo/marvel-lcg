# M.O.D.O.K.

<a id="q-if-m-o-d-o-k-103a-b-is-defeated-by-overkill-damage-from-an-attack-on-an-adaptoid-113-in-what-order-do-m-o-d-o-k-s-forced-interrupt-and-the-adaptoid-s-when-defeated-ability-resolve"></a>
## Q. If M.O.D.O.K. (103A-B) is defeated by overkill damage from an attack on an Adaptoid (113), in what order do M.O.D.O.K.’s “Forced Interrupt” and the Adaptoid’s “When Defeated” ability resolve?

A. Overkill damage is simultaneous with the damage from the attack, so the “Forced Interrupt” on M.O.D.O.K. resolves first because it uses the word “would.” Then, the “When Defeated” ability of the Adaptoid resolves.

<a id="q-what-happens-if-an-ally-105b-108b-leaves-play-and-there-are-no-cards-in-the-holding-cell-deck"></a>
## Q. What happens if an ally (105B-108B) leaves play and there are no cards in the Holding Cell deck?

A. The ally that leaves play flips over and becomes the only card in the Holding Cell deck. Resolve the “enters play” text on its Holding Cell side by placing lock counters on it.
