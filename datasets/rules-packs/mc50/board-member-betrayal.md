# BOARD MEMBER BETRAYAL

A.I.M. constantly hunts for more dirt on the board members—or opportunties to fabricate it! The A.I.M. Interference cards represent these efforts and add additional secrets to each board member when revealed. If the players are not careful, a board member can be turned from a passive obstacle into an active threat. If a board member ever has four secrets on it (three in expert mode), that board member permanently turns against the heroes, flipping to its attachment side to empower the villain and punish the players each time a secret is placed on it.
