# BLACK WIDOW

<a id="q-if-i-attack-black-widow-64-66-to-remove-a-stunned-status-card-do-i-trigger-her-effect"></a>
## Q. If I attack Black Widow (64-66) to remove a stunned status card, do I trigger her effect?

A. No, the stunned status replaces the attack before Black Widow’s “Forced Interrupt” triggers.
