# BARON ZEMO

<a id="q-how-does-the-reluctant-foe-attachment-171-work"></a>
## Q. How does the Reluctant Foe attachment (171) work?

A. Reluctant Foe causes you to choose an identity card from a Marvel Champions hero you are not using in your current game whose title does not match the title of a character in play and attach Reluctant Foe to that identity card with its hero side faceup. That hero becomes a minion with the villainous keyword, SCH equal to its THW value, and hit points equal to its starting hit point value. Its DEF value has no effect. When you defeat that minion, both the identity card and Reluctant Foe are removed from the game.
