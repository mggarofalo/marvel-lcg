# PREPARE THE EVIDENCE

During setup, when you are instructed to prepare the evidence: If you are not playing in campaign mode, follow the steps under “Preparing the Evidence” on page 5. If you are playing in campaign mode, simply place the A.I.M. and S.H.I.E.L.D. envelopes within reach.
