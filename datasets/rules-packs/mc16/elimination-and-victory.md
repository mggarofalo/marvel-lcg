# ELIMINATION AND VICTORY

In an expert campaign, if a player is defeated during a scenario that their teammates go on to win, the defeated player does not participate in the Victory steps of that scenario. However, that player can rejoin their teammates for the next scenario, healing their identity to its printed hit point value.
