# AGGRESSION

A cybernetically-enhanced super-genius raccoon with a knack for tactical warfare, Rocket Raccoon excels at scavenging parts and building highly dangerous weaponry. The more weapons Rocket Raccoon has in play, the deadlier he becomes, able to Reload his weapons for maximum mayhem, or keep his weapons charged up by using his handy Battery Pack. And when one of his weapons finally does run dry or he finds a weapon that is more suited to the mission he’s on, change to alter-ego form to trade a weapon in for something bigger and better. Using the Aggression aspect, go Looking for Trouble to hunt down minions, blast them away using a powerful Hand Cannon, and Follow Through with extra excess damage.
