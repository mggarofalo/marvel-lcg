# DECK CUSTOMIZATION RESTRICTIONS

Once a player starts an expert campaign, they cannot add, remove, or change the aspect and/or basic cards in their deck (following the deck customization rules) for the remainder of the campaign. However, campaign-specific cards can be added to their deck as per the campaign rules.
