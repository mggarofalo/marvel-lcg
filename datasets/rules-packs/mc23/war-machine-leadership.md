# WAR MACHINE // LEADERSHIP

The War Machine armor is the most heavily-armed fighting system in S.H.I.E.L.D.’s arsenal. Use its ammo counters to pay for a powerful War Machine event like Full Auto and deal a massive amount of damage to the villain. Then, flip to alter-ego form to shuffle that event back into your deck and reload your ammo counters. Play the Upgraded Chassis to gain a tough status card each time you change to hero form! With the Leadership aspect, put powerful allies like Black Panther into play with a well-timed Sneak Attack. Activate Command Team to use that ally’s basic powers multiple times before discarding them with a Save the Day to thwart the villain’s scheme!
