# STORM // LEADERSHIP

Storm begins the game with one of four supports in play. Summon a Thunderstorm to increase everyone’s ATK, then play Lightning Strike to deal extra damage to an enemy. Use Storm’s “Weather Control” ability to swap your support for Hurricane to remove threat from a scheme and gain retaliate 1 before the villain phase begins. With the leadership aspect, play “To Me, My X-Men!” to put powerful allies such as Havok into play. Then, use Utopia’s ability to ready an character. Strengthen your team even further by playing the Uncanny X-Men support.
