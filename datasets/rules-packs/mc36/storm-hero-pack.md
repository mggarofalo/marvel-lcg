# STORM HERO PACK

<a id="the-weather-deck"></a>
## The Weather Deck

Storm’s mutant power allows her to control the weather. To represent this incredible ability, Storm begins each game with a special, four-card “ deck” in addition to her player deck. To create the deck, shuffle all four of Storm’s support cards together (Clear Skies, Hurricane, Thunderstorm, and Blizzard). Then, place the deck facedown next to your identity card. Storm’s Setup ability requires you to choose one of the supports in the deck and put it into play. Each one has a constant ability that affects each character in play (friendly and enemy), so be sure to consider that when deciding which one to play. Each support also has the permanent keyword which prevents it from leaving play. However, Storm may swap the support in play with a different one from the deck using her Weather Control ability or the Weather Goddess event. New Icon: Amplify Icon () The amplify icon increases the number of boost icons on boost cards. When a boost card is turned faceup during an enemy activation, add one additional boost icon to that card for each amplify icon in play.

<a id="new-keyword-permanent"></a>
## New Keyword: Permanent

A card with permanent cannot be defeated, leave play, or have any part of its text box blanked. This rule does not apply to cards within the same set as the card with permanent (hero set, scenario set, or modular set).

<a id="new-keyword-piercing"></a>
## New Keyword: Piercing

An attack with piercing discards any tough status cards from the target of the attack before dealing damage.

<a id="new-keyword-stalwart"></a>
## New Keyword: Stalwart

A character with stalwart cannot be stunned or confused.
