# WOLVERINE // AGGRESSION

Wolverine begins the game with Wolverine’s Claws in play. Use his claws to play an event for free by taking damage equal to its cost. If you use Wolverine’s Claws to play Lunging Strike, the attack gains overkill and piercing. Wolverine’s “Healing Factor” ability will heal 2 damage from him at the start of each player phase. With the Aggression aspect, play the Warrior Skill upgrade and use its warrior counters to make events like Outta My Way! deal 1 extra damage. Use Aggressive Energy to pay for that to have it deal another damage, bringing its total to +2 damage.
