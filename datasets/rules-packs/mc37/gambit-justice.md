# GAMBIT // JUSTICE

Gambit’s strategy revolves around spending charge counters to increase the amount of damage dealt by your events. Use Gambit’s “Charge de Card” ability and his Molecular Acceleration resource to place charge counters on him. Then, trigger Gambit’s “Throw de Card” ability when you play Royal Flush to increase each instance of damage dealt by that attack. If there’s too much threat on the main scheme, flip to alter-ego form and use Creole Charmer or Remy LeBeau’s “Thief Extraordinaire” ability to remove it. With the justice aspect, use Breaking and Entering to remove threat while in alter-ego form. Pay for the event with Passion for Justice and use the Operative Skill upgrade to remove up to 2 additional threat!
