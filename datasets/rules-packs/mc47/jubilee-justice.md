# JUBILEE // JUSTICE

Enemies often misjudge Jubilee to their ruin as she surprises them with her explosive plasmoid bursts. Use different resource types to pay for Firecracker and Flash of Light to stun and confuse enemies in addition to dealing damage and removing threat. Play and complete the Shopping Spree side scheme to find Jubilee’s Coat and Jubilee’s Sunglasses to boost her power level even higher! With the Justice and Basic cards, take advantage of the different resource types to unlock different and powerful abilities. Play Multitalented to deal damage, remove threat, and/or heal damage depending on what combination of resources you spend. Spend as many different resources as you can to play Three Steps Ahead to remove more threat from a scheme for each resource icon used!
