# THOR // AGGRESSION

Thor loves the thrill of fight, and is always excited to smash through hordes of enemies. Use the mythic Mjolnir to boost Thor’s attack power and mow down minions, or blast through the mightiest of foes with a forceful Hammer Throw. If the pressure of combat becomes overwhelming, call down a super-charged Lightning Strike to clear the area. With the Aggression aspect, equip Thor with the legendary Jarnbjorn, then crush enemies with a powerful Mean Swing. And once the battle is over and the dust has settled, brag of Thor’s greatest achievements at the Hall of Heroes.
