# CAMPAIGN LOG

The campaign log found on the back cover of this rulebook tracks the progress of the entire campaign. At the end of each scenario, the players record their results by entering the required information in the campaign log. A printable copy of the campaign log may be found on our support page at: www.fantasyflightgames.com/marvel-champions
