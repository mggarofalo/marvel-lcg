# RULES CLARIFICATION

Q. When I put an environment card into play at the beginning of the Absorbing Man scenario, do I trigger the surge keyword? A. No. The surge keyword only triggers when the card it appears on is revealed. Putting a card into play is not the same as revealing a card.
