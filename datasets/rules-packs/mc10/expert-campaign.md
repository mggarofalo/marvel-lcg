# EXPERT CAMPAIGN

For players who want a more challenging campaign experience, The Rise of Red Skull expansion comes with everything you need to play an expert campaign. The rules for this alternate play format are found on page 17.

Some setup and victory instructions are preceded by Expert Campaign Only. Players should ignore these instructions unless they are playing an expert campaign.
