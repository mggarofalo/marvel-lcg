# Campaign-Only Cards

Cards 155–162 are player cards that were created specifically for use in The Rise of Red Skull campaign. These cards cannot be included in any player’s deck unless they are playing The Rise of Red Skull campaign and the players were directed to add them to their decks by the Campaign Instructions.
