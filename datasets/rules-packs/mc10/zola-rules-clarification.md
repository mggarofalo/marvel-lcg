# RULES CLARIFICATION

Q. What does it mean when a card is removed from the campaign log? A. When a card is removed from the campaign log, cross it out of the campaign log. That card is no longer part of the campaign and cannot be included in any deck for the remainder of the campaign. Q. If my ally is removed from the campaign at the end of Zola’s scenario, what do I do? A. If your ally was removed from the campaign, that ally is removed from your deck for the rest of the campaign. If this causes your deck to fall below the minimum number of cards, then you must add a card to your deck.
