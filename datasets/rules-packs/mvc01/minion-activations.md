# MINION ACTIVATIONS

The villain activates once against each player, in player order. After the villain activates against a player, each minion engaged with that player also activates. These activations resolve differently based on the player’s character form. If the player is in their alter-ego form, the villain or minion schemes. If the player is in their hero form, the villain or minion attacks. After the villain completes its activation for a player, it activates for the next player (in player order), continuing until it has activated for all players. Strategy Tip: Your character's form (hero or alter-ego)
