# HERO FORM: ENEMIES ATTACK

If the player is in hero form, the villain (and each minion engaged with that player) attacks. To resolve this activation, perform the following steps: Give the villain a facedown card from the encounter deck. This is the villain’s boost card for this activation. The attacked player chooses if they want to defend against the attack. To defend, the player must either exhaust their hero or exhaust an ally they control. If the attacked player does not defend, any other player may defend against the attack by exhausting their hero or an ally they control. Turn the boost card face up. For each boost icon at the bottom-right of the card, the villain gets +1 ATK for this activation.
