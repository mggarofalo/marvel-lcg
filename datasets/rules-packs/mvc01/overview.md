# OVERVIEW

Marvel ChaMpions: The Card GaMe is a cooperative card game for one to four players. Each player takes the role of one Marvel hero, playing the game as that character and their alter-ego. In the game, the players work together to defeat a villain (controlled by the game) who is trying to complete an evil scheme.
