# ABILITIES

In addition to action abilities, which a player can trigger during their turn (or by request during another player’s turn), there are a few other types of triggered abilities in the game: Interrupts, Responses, and Resource abilities. Using these types of triggered abilities is optional, unless the bold ability trigger is preceded by the word “Forced.” Each copy of a triggered ability may be used once each time the situation described by its text occurs. “Resource” abilities may be used anytime the player who controls the ability is generating resources to pay a cost.
