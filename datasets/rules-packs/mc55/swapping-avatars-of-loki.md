# SWAPPING AVATARS OF LOKI

When a card effect instructs the players to “swap an villain with a random set-aside villain,” they should replace the villain in play with one of the other set-aside versions of the villain. This does not cause any villain to leave play, enter play, or be revealed. Sustained damage (on the villain’s hit point dial), attachments, status cards, counters, and tokens on the villain should be transferred to the new version of the villain that was swapped in. The villain that was swapped out should be set aside with the other remaining set-aside versions of the villain.
