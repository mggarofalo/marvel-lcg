# SHATTERING THE ILLUSION

When an villain would be defeated and flips to its Fading Figment side, players are instructed to “shatter the illusion” by following the steps found on the set-aside Shatter the Illusion rules card. Follow these steps in order, resolving each fully before proceeding to the next step in the sequence, and resolve all such steps before the second sentence of the Fading Figment’s “When Revealed” ability.
