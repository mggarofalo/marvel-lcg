# EPIC MULTIPLAYER MODE

To play in Epic Multiplayer Mode, designate one person to be the “event organizer” (see “Event Organizer” on page 14). Then, split the players into groups, with each group containing between 1 and 4 players (each group will need its own copy of the Trickster Takeover Scenario Pack). While it is possible to play with unevenly numbered groups of players, it is recommended that players be split between groups as evenly as possible.
