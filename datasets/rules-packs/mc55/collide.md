# COLLIDE

The villain Loki, God of Lies, and main scheme Worlds Collide are placed together in their own game area, separate from any players’ game area. As such, these cards can only be affected by abilities and game effects in other game areas that refer to them by title. They cannot be affected by any ability that refers to “the villain” or “the main scheme,” and Worlds Collide cannot have acceleration tokens placed on it.
