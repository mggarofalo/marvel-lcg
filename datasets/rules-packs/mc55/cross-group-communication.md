# CROSS-GROUP COMMUNICATION

environments are beneficial cards in each group’s game area. During play, some encounter card abilities may allow players to choose a group within their pod, then put a number of synergy counters on one of that group’s environments. When choosing a group, coordination is key and cross-group communication is highly encouraged. Each environment offers a different, powerful effect, so knowing which group needs help can alter the course of the game!
