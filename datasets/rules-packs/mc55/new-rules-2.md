# NEW RULES

The God of Lies scenario can be played in one of two ways: with a single group of 1–4 players in Single Group Mode, or with any number of players split into groups of 1–4 players each in Epic Multiplayer Mode. Although each mode functions similarly, there is one key difference: In Single Group Mode, one group fights Loki, God of Lies and his various versions. In Epic Multiplayer Mode, multiple groups fight one Loki, God of Lies together while facing off against their own versions in their group’s game area. Pages 10–18 include special rules pertaining to Single Group Mode and Epic Multiplayer Mode, while pages 19–21 include additional rules and clarifications for both versions of the scenario.
