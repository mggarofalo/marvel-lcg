# GOD OF LIES SCENARIO

<a id="q-when-an-villain-flips-to-its-fading-figment-side-who-resolves-the-text"></a>
## Q. When an villain flips to its Fading Figment side, who resolves the text?

The first player. If an encounter card requires a card ability to be resolved, a game function to be performed, or a choice to be made but does not specify which player should act, the first player does so.

<a id="q-if-the-second-bulleted-effect-on-the-stories-and-lies-treachery-causes-an-villain-to-initiate-an-attack-against-me-while-my-identity-is-in-alter-ego-form-what-happens"></a>
## Q. If the second bulleted effect on the Stories and Lies treachery causes an villain to initiate an attack against me while my identity is in alter-ego form, what happens?

Although attacks against identities in alter-ego form are uncommon, they may still occur. If Stories and Lies initiates an attack against you while your identity is in alter-ego form, resolve the steps of the attack as usual.
