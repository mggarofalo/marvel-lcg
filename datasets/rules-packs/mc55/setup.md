# SETUP

To play in Single Group Mode, follow standard setup instructions. During the “Resolve Scenario Setup” step, first resolve the “Setup” ability on the Worlds Collide (A) main scheme, then resolve the “Setup” ability on the Mischief and Mayhem (1A) main scheme. Although the Loki, God of Lies villain and the Worlds Collide main scheme cannot be interacted with by players, both cards are important for the win/loss conditions of the scenario. As such, keep the Loki, God of Lies villain, his hit point dial, and the Worlds Collide main scheme in a separate game area where they are easily within reach. These cards are not considered in your game area and can only be affected by cards that refer to them by name. By contrast, the villain, his hit point dial, and the Mischief and Mayhem main scheme should be front and center as these are the villain and main scheme that players can interact with as normal.
