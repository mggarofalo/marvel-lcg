# ENCHANTMENT ATTACHMENTS

attachments are double-sided attachments that are dealt to each player during setup. The front side of each attachment is the same (Hypnotic Gaze), while the back side of each has a different title starting with “Trance,” and a unique ability. Players cannot look

<a id="at-the-trance-side-of-any-attachment-until-it-flips-through-hypnotic-gaze-s-ability"></a>
## at the “Trance” side of any attachment until it flips through Hypnotic Gaze’s ability.

During the game, a Hypnotic Gaze may flip to its “Trance” side. When the “Trance” side is revealed this way, trigger its “When Revealed” ability as normal.
