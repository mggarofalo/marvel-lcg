# FORCED ACTIONS

Some attachments have a “Forced Action” triggered ability. A player may perform a forced action at any time during the player phase they could normally perform an action, but the player phase cannot end until all possible forced actions among all players have been performed. If a card with a Forced Action ability is discarded, or the cost cannot otherwise be paid (such as by the card becoming exhausted when the Forced Action ability’s cost includes exhausting the card), then the action cannot be performed and the players are free to end the player phase as normal.
