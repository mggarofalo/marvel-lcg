# SP//DR // PROTECTION

SP//dr’s main focus is powering up her SP//dr Suit through upgrades. Ask Aunt May & Uncle Ben for assistance in building the Suit, or use All Systems Go to search for the missing pieces. Once the Suit is primed and ready, utilize Sync Ratio to stun and damage an enemy with a Web-Trap, or jump into the action with Rapid Deployment and remove threat from multiple schemes. With the Protection aspect, call upon the mysterious Daredevil to take down minions, deploy a well-timed Forcefield Generator to stop powerful attacks, or Repurpose the Forcefield Generator for an explosive turn.
