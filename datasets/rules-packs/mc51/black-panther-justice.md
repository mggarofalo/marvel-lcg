# BLACK PANTHER // JUSTICE

Shuri’s playstyle centers on her four upgrades. Use her “Inventor” ability on her alter-ego form to ensure you have the upgrades you need, then change to hero form and use Black Panther’s hero ability and events to trigger their “Special” abilities. Each of her upgrades can be discarded when used to gain powerful effects. Use Aja-Adanna to shuffle them back into your deck so that Shuri can build them again. With the Justice aspect, Infiltration can remove a lot of threat cheaply. Bring any minions you encounter Redemption through a Show of Empathy, or lock them up on The Raft. Your Basic allies empower each other with their “Special” abilities, while the Dora Milaje support card keeps them in the fight.
