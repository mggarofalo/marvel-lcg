# ANT-MAN HERO PACK

<a id="foldable-cards"></a>
## Foldable Cards

Scott Lang/Ant-Man’s identity card is a foldable, “three-sided” card. One side is his alter-ego form, one side is his hero form, and the inside of the card is his hero form. Changing form with a three-sided card follows the standard rules for changing form found in the Rules Reference.

<a id="new-keyword-team-up"></a>
## New Keyword: Team-Up

The team-up keyword names two characters. To include a card with the team-up keyword in your deck, your chosen identity must match one of the named characters. Additionally, a card with the team-up keyword cannot be played unless both of the named characters (hero or ally) are in play.

<a id="rules-clarifications"></a>
## Rules Clarifications

A: Scott Lang/Ant-Man can change from alter-ego form to either hero form, from either hero form to alter-ego form, or from one hero form to the other hero form.
