# ANT-MAN // LEADERSHIP

Ant-Man has the exciting ability to shrink to hero form or grow to hero form, using his size alteration capabilities to keep enemies on their toes. Changing to hero form allows Ant-Man to remove threat from a scheme, while changing to hero form lets Ant-Man deal some easy damage. Many of Ant-Man’s cards, such as Giant Stomp or Hive Mind, can only be played in one hero form or another, meaning that changing between forms often and optimizing the use of these effects is the key to Ant-Man’s success. With the Leadership aspect, Call for Aid to recruit allies like Giant-Man or Ronin to the fight against evil, then augment their abilities using a Reinforced Suit and Power Gloves. And using a little bit of Moxie, empower Ant-Man after changing form for an action-packed turn!
