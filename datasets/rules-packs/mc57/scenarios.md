# SCENARIOS

The Synthezoid Smackdown pack comes with two preconstructed scenarios ready to play right out of the box. Here is an introduction to both the registration and resistance scenarios. Leader Deck: She-Hulk (I), She-Hulk (II) Main Scheme Deck: Enforce the Law, Mighty Avengers Encounter Deck: She-Hulk, S.H.I.E.L.D. Ops, Thunderbolts, Taskmaster, Deadly Duo, and Standard sets. (The Standard set can be found in the Marvel Champions core set.) Leader Deck: Vision (I), Vision (II) Main Scheme Deck: Protect Secret Identities, Expose Overreach Encounter Deck: Vision, Young Avengers, Scarlet Twins, Moon Knight, Royal Guard, and Standard sets. (The Standard set can be found in the Marvel Champions core set.)
