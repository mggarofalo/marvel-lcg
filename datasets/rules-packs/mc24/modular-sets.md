# MODULAR SETS

In addition to one scenario, The Hood Scenario Pack contains nine modular encounter sets—Beasty Boys, Brothers Grimm, Crossfire’s Crew, Mister Hyde, Ransacked Armory, Sinister Syndicate, State of Emergency, Streets of Mayhem, and Wrecking Crew. To add variety and unpredictability to any game of Marvel Champions, a number of modular encounter sets can be added to (and/or removed from) a scenario. When adding a modular set, players may either choose the set they would like to play against (from this product or other Marvel Champions products), or (to increase unpredictability) they can select one at random from all available modular sets and shuffle the set into the encounter deck without looking at it.
