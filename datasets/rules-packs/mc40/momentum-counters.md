# MOMENTUM COUNTERS

Various effects in this scenario place momentum counters on Juggernaut, which boost his ATK. The players can remove these counters via the hero action on Juggernaut’s Helmet. When a stage of Juggernaut is defeated, all momentum counters on him carry over to the next villain stage.
