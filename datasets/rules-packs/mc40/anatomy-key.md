# ANATOMY KEY

Title. The name of the card. Card Type. Indicates how the card behaves or may be used in the game. Ability. The card’s specialized means of interacting with the game. Cost. The resource cost to play the card. Resources. The resources the card generates when it is discarded from hand. Starting Threat. The amount of threat placed on the side scheme when it enters play. Deckbuilding Classification. Indicates if the card is exclusive to a hero, belongs to an aspect, or is a basic card. Collector Information. Indicates the card’s product of origin and card number within that product.
