# ELIMINATION AND VICTORY

In an expert campaign, if a player is defeated during a scenario that their teammates go on to win, the defeated player does not participate in the Victory steps of that scenario. However, during the Setup instructions of the next scenario, the defeated player can rejoin their teammates for the next scenario by following that scenario’s setup instructions for healing their identity to its full hit points.
