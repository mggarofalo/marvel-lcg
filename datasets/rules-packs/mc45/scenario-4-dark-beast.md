# SCENARIO #4 – DARK BEAST

Villain Deck: Dark Beast (I), Dark Beast (II) Main Scheme Deck: Dark Beast’s Bogus Journey Encounter Deck: Dark Beast, Blue Moon, Genosha, Savage Land, Dystopian Nightmare, and Standard sets. (The Standard set can be found in the Marvel Champions core set.) Shuffle the Age of Apocalypse modular set into the encounter deck. Randomly select one of the available side schemes and follow the Setup directions for it in the campaign log. Randomly select one of the available minions and add it to the mission area. Put the double-sided Mission Rules card into play next to it. The first player takes control of the Mission Team (171A) support card, side faceup. Each player searches their deck for an ally and adds it to their hand. (This card counts towards your hand size.) Expert Campaign Only: Set each player’s hit points to their remaining hit point value recorded in the campaign log for the previous scenario. Expert Campaign Only: Each player may place 3 threat on the side scheme to heal their identity to its full hit point value. Strike the side scheme from the campaign log. If the side scheme was defeated, follow the “Defeated” instructions for that side scheme in the campaign log. If the side scheme was not defeated, follow the “Not Defeated” instructions for that side scheme in the campaign log. If the minion was defeated, strike its name from the campaign log Expert Campaign Only: Record each identity’s remaining hit points in the campaign log.
