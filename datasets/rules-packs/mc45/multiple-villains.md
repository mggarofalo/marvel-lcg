# MULTIPLE VILLAINS

The Four Horsemen scenario has four different villains: War (81), Famine (82), Pestilence (83), and Death (84). All four villains are revealed in random order and put into play in a row when setting up the scenario. Give each villain a hit point dial and set it to their starting hit points. If you do not have enough hit point dials, you can use dice, counters, pen and paper, or some other method to track each villain’s hit points.
