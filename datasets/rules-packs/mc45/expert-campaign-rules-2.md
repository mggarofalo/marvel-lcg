# EXPERT CAMPAIGN RULES

For players who want an even more challenging campaign experience, the Age of Apocalypse expansion comes with everything you need to play an expert campaign. Some Setup and Victory instructions are preceded by Expert Campaign Only. Ignore these instructions unless you are playing an expert campaign.
