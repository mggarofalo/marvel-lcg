# NEW RULES

<a id="find"></a>
## “Find”

When you are instructed to find a card, search each area where the card could be found (play area, set-aside area, player deck, discard pile, encounter deck, etc). Players should not unnecessarily search game areas if they know where the card they are looking for can be found. Do NOT look at facedown encounter cards currently dealt to players.
