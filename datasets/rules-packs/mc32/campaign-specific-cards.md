# CAMPAIGN-SPECIFIC CARDS

During a campaign, campaign-specific cards may be added to encounter decks and player decks. These cards cannot be included in any deck unless playing the Mutant Genesis campaign and the players are directed to add them to a deck by the Campaign Instructions.
