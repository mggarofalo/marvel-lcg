# NEW RULES

<a id="additional-forms"></a>
## Additional Forms

Some heroes come with cards that grant them additional forms, such as Shadowcat’s “Phase form.” These forms are in addition to your identity’s alter-ego and hero forms and come with their own conditions for changing into them. When an identity changes an additional form, it does not count against the once-per-turn-limit on flipping from hero to alter-ego (or vice versa), but it does count as changing forms for the purpose of triggering card effects such as Ready to Rumble (51).
