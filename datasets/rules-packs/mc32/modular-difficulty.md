# MODULAR DIFFICULTY

If players wish to modify the difficulty of the Sabretooth scenario, they may place damage on Robert Kelly during setup. This extra damage represents Sabretooth’s initial attack on the senator. The amount of damage placed is up to the players as a group, but listed below are some recommendations for each difficulty mode: Skirmish Mode: Place 0 damage. Standard Mode: Place 1 damage. Expert Mode: Place 2 damage. Heroic Mode: Place 3 damage.
