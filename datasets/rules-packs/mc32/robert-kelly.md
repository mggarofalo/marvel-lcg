# ROBERT KELLY

The Sabretooth scenario tells the story of the X-Men protecting Robert Kelly (66) from Sabretooth’s assassination attempt. To help bring the story to life, the scenario features an ally version of Robert Kelly. It has the text: “Forced Interrupt: When an enemy resolves an undefended attack against you, deal that damage to Robert Kelly.”
