# PERSISTENT DAMAGE

While playing the Fear No Evil campaign at the expert level, each player must record their remaining hit points in the campaign log after each game. This determines each player’s starting hit points for the next scenario. If a player’s remaining hit points is greater than their base hit points, record their base hit points in the campaign log instead.
