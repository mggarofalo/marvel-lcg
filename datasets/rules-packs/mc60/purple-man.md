# PURPLE MAN

To set up Purple Man, use Purple Man (I) and (II). (Use Purple Man (II) and (III) instead for expert mode.) Purple Man’s stats may be low, but that’s because he makes others do his dirty work. Each of his cards gains a “Boost” ability that deals that card as a facedown encounter card after the activation. Each card has a star () in the boost field to remind you of this. Purple Man’s encounter set includes several minions—mind-controlled citizens who punish you if you defeat them. Purple Man gives these minions guard (and villainous and patrol at higher stages). However, since they all have the vulnerable keyword, they can be safely discarded if stunned or confused.
