# OUT FRONT/ALONGSIDE

This double-sided attachment on the villain indicates whether the heroes have kept up with the villain and are Alongside them, or if they’re getting away and Out Front. This attachment starts the game on the Out Front side. While the villain is Out Front, any damage you would deal to them instead removes that amount of threat from the main scheme. (If the villain has a tough status card, they keep that card because the damage is not actually dealt to them. Similarly, if the villain has a attachment from the Drive modular set, no damage is placed on that card.) If there is no threat on the main scheme, Out Front flips to Alongside. This triggers the “When Revealed” effect on Alongside, dealing damage to both the villain and the heroes as they collide at high speed. While you are Alongside, the attachment has no further effect. The attachment flips back to Out Front at the end of each round in which there is threat on the main scheme.
