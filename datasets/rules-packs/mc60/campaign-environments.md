# CAMPAIGN ENVIRONMENTS

Each interchangeable main scheme has a corresponding environment card. These cards are double-sided, with a side and a side. The side indicates what benefits the players gain by playing and winning that scenario. The side indicates what penalties the players suffer if that scenario has failed. Players may look at both sides of each of these cards when choosing which scenario to play. Once a scenario is completed or failed, the players begin each subsequent game with that scenario’s environment in play on its appropriate side.
