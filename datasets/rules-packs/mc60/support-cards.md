# SUPPORT CARDS

In this scenario, each player starts the game with a random support under their control. Each of these supports has the “Uses (3 stamina counters)” keyword. As usual, it enters play with 3 stamina counters on it and, if all of those counters are removed, the support is discarded. In this scenario, the main scheme indicates that if a support leaves play, the players lose the game. Thus, it is imperative that the players protect their supports from leaving play. The supports can lose stamina counters in several ways. First, each of their abilities spends a stamina counter when used. Second, the main scheme removes 1 of their stamina counters after an undefended enemy attack unless the players place threat on the main scheme. And finally, some encounter cards can remove stamina counters. Fortunately, the players have the ability on the Daily Bugle environment to restock stamina counters should their support get low.
