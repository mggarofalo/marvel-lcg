# ART PIECES

This scenario includes four art pieces, each represented by an attachment. One of these art pieces, chosen at random by stage 1A of the main scheme, begins in play attached to the villain. Leaving these attachments on the villain can quicken the completion of the main scheme, both because these art pieces increase the attached character’s SCH or THW value and because the main scheme gets an additional 1 threat added to it during step 1 of each villain phase for each attachment on the villain. A player can take an art piece away from the villain by triggering the Hero Action on the art piece, which attaches the art piece to their hero. Three of the four pieces give a status card to a character when they first attach to that character, while the fourth grants the attached character the stalwart keyword, removing any stunned or confused status cards from that character. The villain can steal an art piece back each time an identity with one attached takes an attack undefended. This can be either a villain attack or a minion attack, but only one art piece is stolen after each undefended attack.
