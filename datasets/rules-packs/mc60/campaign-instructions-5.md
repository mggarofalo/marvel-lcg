# CAMPAIGN INSTRUCTIONS

Remove 1 stamina counter from each Support for each check mark in the campaign log beside “Stop the Presses!” If Disturbed Psyche (112) is in play and has at least 2 threat on it, check the “Trust Established?” box in the campaign log. If the Typhoid Mary/Bloody Mary ally (210) is in the victory display, check the “Mary Defeated?” box in the campaign log. Record the title of each unique ally and support that was removed from the game in the campaign log. Check the “Completed” box in the campaign log for Stop the Presses!. Go to “Campaign Setup” on page 9. Expert Campaign Only: Progress “Stop the Presses!” in the campaign log. Go to “Campaign Setup” on page 9.
