# CAMPAIGN MODE RULES

Each of the scenarios in this expansion can be played individually as a standalone adventure, or together with the other scenarios as part of one overarching campaign. Campaign mode combines five of the six scenarios in the Fear No Evil expansion into one epic experience, where the outcome of each game impacts the next scenario. Unlike other Marvel Champions campaigns, the order of these scenarios is not fixed. Instead, the players choose which scenario to play next after each game. To begin a campaign, the players start by choosing their heroes. Each player must use their chosen identity for the entire campaign, but they are free to change aspects and alter the contents of their deck between scenarios, following the deck customization rules in the Marvel Champions Rules Reference.
