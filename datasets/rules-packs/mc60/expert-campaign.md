# EXPERT CAMPAIGN

For players who want an even more challenging campaign experience, the Fear No Evil expansion comes with everything you need to play an expert campaign. Some Setup and Victory instructions are preceded by Expert Campaign Only. Ignore these instructions unless you are playing an expert campaign.
