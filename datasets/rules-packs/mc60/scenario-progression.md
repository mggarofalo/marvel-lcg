# SCENARIO PROGRESSION

Before choosing each scenario—including the first—two of the scenarios that haven’t been resolved yet are chosen at random. These scenarios progress (this is explained in detail in campaign setup) as the villain continues with their plot while the heroes are occupied elsewhere. If a scenario progresses too much, the players automatically fail that scenario and receive a penalty for the rest of the campaign. Losing a scenario does not automatically cause it to fail and it may be able to be attempted again. Conversely, winning a scenario earns the players a benefit for the rest of the campaign. Once all five interchangeable main schemes are either Completed or Failed, the players take on Kingpin in a final showdown! Before playing each scenario in campaign mode, follow the instructions in the Campaign Setup section on page 9. When the game ends, if the players won, follow that scenario’s victory instructions in the order they are listed. If the players lost, follow that scenario’s defeat instructions instead.
