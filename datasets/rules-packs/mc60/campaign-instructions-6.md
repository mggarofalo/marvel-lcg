# CAMPAIGN INSTRUCTIONS

If the number of environments in play is at least: 3, give each minion in play a tough status card. 4, find and reveal the James Wesley attachment. Standard Mode Only: Replay the scenario. Expert Campaign Only: If there is at least one environment on its side, choose and flip one to its side and replay the scenario. Otherwise, the players lose the campaign. The players take down Kingpin and win the campaign!
