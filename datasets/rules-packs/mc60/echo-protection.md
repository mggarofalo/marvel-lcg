# ECHO // PROTECTION

Echo’s photographic reflexes allow her to perfectly recreate any physical movement she sees. Any time a player plays an aspect or basic event, Echo can tuck it under her identity card. She can then discard one of the six copies of her event, Photographic Reflexes, to play a tucked event with its cost reduced by two. Maya Lopez’s alter-ego ability and Study the Tape events both help ensure you’ll always have an event to “echo.” Muscle Memory lets you return a tucked card to hand if you have no Photographic Reflexes to play it with, and Improvisation gives you even more benefits for playing events. The protection half of Echo’s deck is chock-full of events for her to echo. Boost Echo’s DEF with Ronin and Innate Reflexes, the latter of which can start in your opening hand thanks to its “starting” keyword. The Best Offense... temporarily boosts your DEF even higher and lets you use it in place of your THW and ATK. Army of One lets Echo ready to get even more value from her boosted stats, and In Harm’s Way can do some serious damage and threat removal. Just be careful playing your Daredevil ally as he will reduce the effectiveness of many of your aspect cards.
