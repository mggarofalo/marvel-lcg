# THE RAFT BREAKOUT

<a id="q-when-baron-zemo-145-leaves-play-what-happens-to-allies-with-threat-on-them"></a>
## Q. When Baron Zemo (145) leaves play, what happens to allies with threat on them?

A. They return to being allies under their owner’s control, but the threat tokens stay. If Baron Zemo enters play again, those allies become minions again (in addition to any ally that Baron Zemo places a threat on).
