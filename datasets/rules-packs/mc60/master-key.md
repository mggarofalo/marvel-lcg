# MASTER KEY

The villain has acquired a key, represented by the Master Key attachment, that can open any prison cell in the Raft. When the villain schemes against a player, if any of the villain’s boost cards is a minion, resolve any boost effects as normal and then deal each such minion to that player as a facedown encounter card instead of discarding it.
