# ELECTRO

To set up Electro, use Electro (I) and (II). (Use Electro (II) and (III) instead for expert mode.) Electro places charge counters on her permanent Electric Charge attachment, and she spends those counters to give herself additional boost cards. She replenishes these charges when she schemes and through various encounter card effects. Conversely, players can remove these counters by spending resources. Energy () resources are double-edged swords as they are more effective at removing charges, but also make players more susceptible to her treacheries.
