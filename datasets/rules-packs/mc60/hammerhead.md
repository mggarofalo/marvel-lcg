# HAMMERHEAD

To set up Hammerhead, use Hammerhead (I) and (II). (Use Hammerhead (II) and (III) instead for expert mode.) Hammerhead is all about stunning his enemies. His “Forced Response” stuns any character that he damages. The “Head First!” treachery and Maggia Underboss minions hit even harder when your hero is stunned. Use tough status cards, your hero’s DEF value, and expendable allies to keep your hero from being stunned.
