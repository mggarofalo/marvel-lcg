# FEATURED KEYWORDS

* Keyword new to this expansion. ** Keyword updated starting with this expansion. Discount X () * When a player plays a card with discount X, its resource cost is reduced by X if that player’s identity has the specified trait. If more than one trait is specified and the player’s identity has at least one of the specified traits, the cost is reduced by X.

<a id="hinder-x"></a>
## Hinder X

When a card with hinder X enters play, place X additional threat on that card.

<a id="patrol"></a>
## Patrol

While a minion with patrol is engaged with a player, that player cannot use cards they control to thwart the main scheme.

<a id="permanent"></a>
## Permanent

A card with permanent cannot be defeated, leave play, or have any part of its text box blanked, except by card abilities in the same set (hero set, scenario set, or modular set).

<a id="piercing"></a>
## Piercing

An attack with piercing discards any tough status cards from the target of the attack before dealing damage.

<a id="prerequisite-form-or"></a>
## Prerequisite (form or ) *

In order for a player to play a card with prerequisite (form) or prerequisite (), their identity must be in the specified form (i.e. Hero or Alter-Ego form) or have the specified trait.

<a id="ranged"></a>
## Ranged

An attack with ranged ignores the retaliate keyword.

<a id="setup"></a>
## Setup

A card with setup begins the game in play.

<a id="stalwart"></a>
## Stalwart

A character with stalwart cannot be stunned or confused. Starting * A card with starting may be added to its owner’s starting hand during setup. Each deck cannot include more than one card with starting. Before drawing their starting hand during player setup, each player with a starting card in their deck may search their deck for that card and add it as the first card of their starting hand. That card does count against their hand size and can be discarded for the mulligan.

<a id="steady"></a>
## Steady

A character with steady can have one additional stunned status card and one additional confused status card. That character is not stunned unless they have two stunned status cards, and is not confused unless they have two confused status cards. After that character’s attack, scheme, or thwart is canceled by a status card effect, remove all status cards of the corresponding type from that character.

<a id="team-up"></a>
## Team-Up

The team-up keyword names two characters. To include a card with team-up in a player’s deck, their chosen identity must match one of the named characters. Additionally, a card with team-up cannot be played unless both of the named characters (identity or ally) are in play.

<a id="teamwork"></a>
## Teamwork ()

After a minion with teamwork enters play and engages a player, if there is at least one other minion that shares the specified trait in play, the minion that just entered play activates against the player it is engaged with. Example: Kate is engaged with Ivan Banionis when Johnny reveals and engages a Tracksuit Bro, who has Teamwork (). Because Ivan Banionis also has the trait, the Tracksuit Bro activates against Johnny.

<a id="temporary"></a>
## Temporary

A card with temporary must be discarded from play at the end of the round.

<a id="victory-x"></a>
## Victory X

When a card with victory X is defeated, place it in the victory display instead of its owner’s discard pile. Villainous ** When a character with villainous uses a basic power, give it a facedown boost card from the top of the encounter deck. When resolving that basic power, turn the boost card faceup, resolve any boost ability on that card, and apply its boost icons to the character’s stats for this use. Discard the boost card after the basic power resolves. Vulnerable ** When a character with vulnerable becomes stunned or confused, that character is immediately discarded. That character is not considered defeated. If the character would be simultaneously defeated by damage and become stunned or confused, the first player chooses whether that character is considered defeated or not.
