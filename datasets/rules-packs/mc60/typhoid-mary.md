# TYPHOID MARY

To set up Typhoid Mary, reveal the Establish Trust side scheme (113A) and the Disturbed Psyche environment (112). Resolve the “Setup” ability on Disturbed Psyche to put the Typhoid Mary (A)/Bloody Mary (A) card into play on a random side. (Use Typhoid Mary (B)/Bloody Mary (B) instead for Expert mode). The villain flips at the end of each villain phase, switching between her Typhoid Mary form and her Bloody Mary form. This means that her attack and thwart will alternate being her higher stat—so plan accordingly. The players do not win this scenario in the usual way (defeating all the villain stages), but instead by getting three tokens on the Disturbed Psyche environment. The players gain tokens either by removing all the threat from Establish Trust or by reducing the villain’s hit points to 0. Establish Trust adds threat tokens to Disturbed Psyche, while the villain’s “Forced Interrupt” adds damage tokens. In campaign play, if Disturbed Psyche has more threat than damage when the players win, they gain access to a Typhoid Mary/Bloody Mary ally for the rest of the campaign.
