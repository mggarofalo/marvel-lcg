# DOUBLE-SIDED VILLAIN

Kingpin is represented by two double-sided villain cards: one for standard mode (A1/A2) and one for expert mode (B1/B2). Kingpin begins the game on his “1” side, which represents him operating from behind the scenes and pulling the strings of the heroes and their nemeses. On his “1” side, Kingpin is untouchable by the players; he cannot be stunned or confused, cannot take damage, and cannot have upgrades attached to him. Whenever he would attack, he schemes instead. In order to confront Kingpin directly, the heroes must first convince the public that they are not to blame for the wave of crime and violence gripping the city. To do this, they must build public support by bringing bad guys to justice. Each time a minion is defeated, a support counter is placed on the Public Support environment. If there are enough support counters there, the heroes can take on Kingpin directly. At the start of the villain phase, Public Support flips Kingpin to his “2” side, advances the main scheme, and flips itself to its side. Once this happens, Kingpin can be attacked directly, though the main scheme gives each minion the guard keyword.
