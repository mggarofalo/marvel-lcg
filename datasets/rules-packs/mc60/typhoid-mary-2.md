# TYPHOID MARY

<a id="q-when-bloody-mary-b-typhoid-mary-b-111-flips-does-her-when-revealed-ability-or-toughness-keyword-resolve"></a>
## Q. When Bloody Mary (B)/Typhoid Mary (B) (111) flips, does her “When Revealed” ability or toughness keyword resolve?

A. Yes, the ability or keyword on her new faceup side resolves.
