# CAMPAIGN INSTRUCTIONS

If there is at least one check mark beside “The Getaway” in the campaign log, find Jackknifed Tanker Truck and reveal it. If there are two check marks, place an additional 1 threat (2 threat instead in Expert mode) on Jackknifed Tanker Truck. If Disturbed Psyche (112) is in play and has at least 2 threat on it, check the “Trust Established?” box in the campaign log. If the Typhoid Mary/Bloody Mary ally (210) is in the victory display, check the “Mary Defeated?” box in the campaign log. Record the title of each unique ally and support that was removed from the game in the campaign log. Check the “Completed” box in the campaign log for The Getaway. Go to “Campaign Setup” on page 9. Expert Campaign Only: Progress “The Getaway” in the campaign log. Go to “Campaign Setup” on page 9.
