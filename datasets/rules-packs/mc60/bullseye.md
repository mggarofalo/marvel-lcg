# BULLSEYE

To set up Bullseye, use Bullseye (I) and (II). (Use Bullseye (II) and (III) instead for expert mode.) Each of Bullseye’s boost cards gets an additional boost icon, which makes him particularly dangerous if he gains extra boost cards by Raise the Stakes or various “Boost” abilities on treachery cards. Many of Bullseye’s encounter cards target either your allies or your supports and can remove them from the game. Note that if this happens during a campaign, those allies and supports are gone for the rest of the campaign! With Bullseye (II), Bullseye’s permanent Adamantium-Laced Spine attachment prevents all but 3 damage from each attack, meaning he is more susceptible to small attacks. If you manage to flip the attachment, make the most of this opportunity with big attacks.
