# CAMPAIGN INSTRUCTIONS

If there is at least one check mark beside “The Raft Breakout” in the campaign log, give each minion in play a tough status card. If there are two check marks, give each of those minions a facedown boost card as well. If Disturbed Psyche (112) is in play and has at least 2 threat on it, check the “Trust Established?” box in the campaign log. If the Typhoid Mary/Bloody Mary ally (210) is in the victory display, check the “Mary Defeated?” box in the campaign log. Record the title of each unique ally and support that was removed from the game in the campaign log. Check the “Completed” box in the campaign log for The Raft Breakout. Go to “Campaign Setup” on page 9. Expert Campaign Only: Progress “The Raft Breakout” in the campaign log. Go to “Campaign Setup” on page 9.
