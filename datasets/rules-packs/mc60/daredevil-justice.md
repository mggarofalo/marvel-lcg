# DAREDEVIL // JUSTICE

Daredevil’s superhuman senses are key to his power. (See Daredevil’s Sense Deck on the next page.) Not only do these upgrades provide powerful abilities to Daredevil when he interacts with the card to which the upgrades are attached, they also power up his events. Even though you can look through the deck at any time, just focus on the top card. Any extra resources that you don’t plan to use for the cards in your hand can be used to play out upgrades. And if you decide you need a specific sense, both Deposition and The Man Without Fear let you find just the upgrade you need. Daredevil’s power may be in his senses, but Matt Murdock’s is in his friends. Foggy Nelson helps deal with threat on turns you are in alter-ego form, while Sister Maggie helps nurse you back to health and Karen Page gets important cards back into your deck. Nelson & Murdock confuses the villain after Matt or Foggy defeats a side scheme. Note that all of Daredevil’s events can be played while in alter-ego form. Daredevil’s aspect cards come with several more upgrades that attach to enemies or schemes to power up his events even further. Know Your Enemy and Stick help you defeat side schemes exactly so you can stun the villain with Stealth Training. Sense cards: Acute Tactility, Enhanced Olfaction, Heightened Hearing, Radar Sense, Superior Taste Daredevil cards: Elektra, Cross Examination (x2), Deposition (x2), Living Lie Detector (x2), Raising Hell, Focus the Senses, Foggy Nelson, Karen Page, Nelson and Murdock, Sister Maggie, Daredevil’s Billy Club, The Man Without Fear Justice cards: Blindspot, Cloak, Dagger, Ghost Rider, Know Your Enemy (x3), De-escalation, Chance Encounter (x3), Legal Trouble (x3), Move in Shadow (x3), Stealth Training (x3) Basic cards: Stick, Contingency Planning (x3), Dance with the Devil Nemesis set: Bullseye, Deadliest Man Alive, Stolen Sai, Eye on the Target (x2) Obligation: Sensory Overload Daredevil’s superhuman senses are represented by his deck. To create the sense deck, shuffle all five of Daredevil’s cards together. Place it faceup next to your identity and you can look through it at any time, but you cannot change the order. The ability on Daredevil’s hero card allows you to play the top card of the deck as if it were in your hand by paying its cost. When a upgrade leaves play, the forced interrupt on both sides of Daredevil’s identity card causes that upgrade to be placed on the bottom of the deck.
