# CAMPAIGN INSTRUCTIONS

Place 1 threat (2 threat in expert mode) on each main scheme for each check mark in the campaign log beside “Protection Racket.” If Disturbed Psyche (112) is in play and has at least 2 threat on it, check the “Trust Established?” box in the campaign log. If the Typhoid Mary/Bloody Mary ally (210) is in the victory display, check the “Mary Defeated?” box in the campaign log. Record the title of each unique ally and support that was removed from the game in the campaign log. Check the “Completed” box in the campaign log for Protection Racket. Go to “Campaign Setup” on page 9. Expert Campaign Only: Progress “Protection Racket” in the campaign log. Go to “Campaign Setup” on page 9.
