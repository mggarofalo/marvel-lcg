# FALCON // LEADERSHIP

Falcon’s hero ability allows you to play with the top card of the encounter deck faceup during the player phase. Many of Falcon’s identity-specific cards gain bonuses based on the number of icons in the boost area of that card, and his “Eagle-Eyed” hero ability allows you to clear a low-boost card to set up one of Falcon’s abilities, or discard a high-boost card in preparation for the villain phase. (Note that “Eagle-Eyed” triggers after you completely resolve an card’s effects, so “Eagle-Eyed” cannot be used to change the resolution of that card.) Use Aerial Recon to choose your encounter card for the round, or to help out a teammate who is under pressure. With the Leadership aspect, assemble your allies into a Flight Squadron and boost their THW or ATK with Cloud 9 and Aero. Give them each a Wingman to watch their six and prevent them from taking consequential damage. Take on the of Captain America to equip Cap’s iconic shield and then bounce it back to your hand to throw damage around the board.
