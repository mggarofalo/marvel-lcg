# LEADERSHIP

With his iconic shield in hand, Captain America is unrelenting in his desire to protect the freedom of others, and is always ready to defend the innocent and retaliate against the guilty. Use a well-timed Shield Toss to take down multiple enemies at once, or rush into the fray with Fearless Determination and save the day! With the Leadership aspect, activate Avengers Tower or Quinjet to call on Captain America’s long-time friend, Falcon, or the always-eager Squirrel Girl. Once the team is prepped and ready, coordinate a powerful attack using Avengers Assemble to take down the villainous forces.
