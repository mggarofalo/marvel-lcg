# PHOENIX // JUSTICE

Phoenix begins the game with the Phoenix Force upgrade in play on its side. Use Phoenix’s “Psionic Bond” ability to spend power counters from Phoenix Force to help pay for powerful cards like Mind Control. But be careful, when you spend the last power counter on Phoenix Force, it flips to its side! While this will make events like Telekinetic Attack hit harder, it may also cause your Burning Hunger obligation to bring Dark Phoenix into play. With the justice aspect, call upon Banshee to help thwart the villain’s schemes. Attach Mission Training to him to boost his THW and hit points. Play Mutant Peacekeepers and exhaust your characters to remove threat from among schemes equal to their combined THW.
