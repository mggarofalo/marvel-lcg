# RULES CLARIFICATION

Q. The “When Revealed” text on stage 1B tells players to put a Spell environment into their play area. Does this trigger its surge keyword? A. No. The surge keyword is only resolved when the card it appears on is revealed. If the card is simply “put into play,” it is not revealed. Q. If Ebony Maw is stunned or confused, does his Forced Interrupt still trigger when he activates against me? A. No. If Ebony Maw is prevented from attacking or scheming by a status card, he removes the status card instead of resolving his activation.
