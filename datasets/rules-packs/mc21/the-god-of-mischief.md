# THE GOD OF MISCHIEF

Loki is the Asgardian god of mischief. To represent the trickster god’s sorcerous powers, Loki does not have ordinary villain stages. Instead, Loki has five different stage-I villain cards. When setting up Loki’s scenario, choose one Loki villain card at random, reveal it and put it into play. Set the remaining four versions of Loki aside, out of play. When the players defeat Loki, instead of revealing the next villain stage, the players reveal one of the set-aside versions of Loki at random.
