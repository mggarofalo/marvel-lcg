# PROHIBITED CARDS

While playing the Sinister Motives campaign, players cannot include the following cards in their player deck unless a campaign rule states otherwise: Venom (Eddie Brock) (190) Symbiote Suit (191) While playing the Sinister Motives campaign, cards from the following modular encounter sets cannot be included in any scenario unless a campaign rule states otherwise: Osborn Tech (147—152) Sinister Assault (158—163)
