# CAMPAIGN-SPECIFIC CARDS

During a campaign, campaign-specific cards may be added to encounter decks and player decks. These cards cannot be included in a deck unless players are playing the Sinister Motives campaign. Cards 182–189 are the campaign-specific player cards in the Sinister Motives campaign and belong to the “Campaign - S.H.I.E.L.D. Tech” deckbuilding classification. Cards 176–180 are campaign-specific encounter cards used during the Sinister Motives campaign and belong to the “Campaign - Community Service” modular encounter sets. Setup instructions for each scenario may direct players to include cards from this set in the scenario.
