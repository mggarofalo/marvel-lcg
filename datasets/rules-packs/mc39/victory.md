# VICTORY:

Record in the campaign log whether the Longshot ally ( 71) is in play. Check off the name of the modular encounter set used in this scenario in the campaign log. Each player may choose one support or upgrade they control with a cost of two or less (three or less if The Champion is on its side) and record it in the campaign log. (Each player will be able to start the following scenarios with their chosen card in play at the cost of additional threat on the main scheme.) Players cannot choose cards without a cost (i.e. that have a cost of “–“). Expert Campaign Only: Record each identity’s remaining hit points in the campaign log.
