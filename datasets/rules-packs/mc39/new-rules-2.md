# NEW RULES

<a id="the-show-deck"></a>
## The Show Deck

Each of the three modular sets included in this scenario includes one environment card. During setup, one of these environments is chosen at random to begin in play, representing the show in which the heroes are currently searching for Spiral. The other two environmenSts are shuffled together with the Cornered! treachery card during setup to form the show deck. The show deck has no discard pile and cannot be affected by player card effects. Players can interact with this deck only through the side scheme The Search for Spiral, which is discussed on the next page.

<a id="double-sided-villain"></a>
## Double-Sided Villain

Each of Spiral’s villain cards are double-sided, with the trait on one side and the trait on the other. Spiral begins the game on her side. This side has an ability that prevents threat from being removed from the main scheme, and prevents Spiral from taking damage.Spiral cannot take damage or be stunned.
