# MODULAR SETS

In addition to three scenarios, the MojoMania scenario pack contains six modular encounter sets that represent different genres of television programming—Crime, Fantasy, Horror, Sci-Fi, Sitcom, and Western. These encounter sets can be used in any scenario to add variety and unpredictability.
