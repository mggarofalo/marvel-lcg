# UESTIONS

<a id="q-if-a-character-s-attack-places-enough-damage-on-stinger-tail-to-discard-it-does-that-character-take-retaliate-damage-from-stinger-tail"></a>
## Q. If a character’s attack places enough damage on Stinger Tail to discard it, does that character take retaliate damage from Stinger Tail?

A. No, Stinger Tail is discarded before its retaliate triggers.

<a id="q-if-both-stinger-tail-and-undercover-mojo-are-in-play-which-card-s-forced-interrupt-triggers"></a>
## Q. If both Stinger Tail and Undercover Mojo are in play, which card’s Forced Interrupt triggers?

A. Both cards have the same trigger, so the first player chooses which one to resolve.

<a id="q-do-the-environment-cards-surge-when-revealed-from-spiral-s-show-deck-or-by-mojo-s-wheel-of-genres"></a>
## Q. Do the environment cards surge when revealed from Spiral’s show deck or by Mojo’s Wheel of Genres?

A. No. In both of these cases, the card was not “revealed from the encounter deck,” so it does not surge.

<a id="q-does-the-forced-interrupt-on-wild-wild-mojo-increase-consequential-damage"></a>
## Q. Does the “Forced Interrupt” on Wild Wild Mojo increase consequential damage?

A. Yes, if an ally takes at least one consequential damage for attacking or thwarting, Wild Wild Mojo increases that consequential damage by one.

<a id="q-how-is-overkill-damage-affected-by-the-forced-interrupt-on-wild-wild-mojo"></a>
## Q. How is overkill damage affected by the “Forced Interrupt” on Wild Wild Mojo?

A. Wild Wild Mojo first increases the initial damage dealt to a minion or ally by one, and then when the excess damage is applied to the villain or identity, Wild Wild Mojo increases it by another one, meaning the total overkill damage dealt is increased by two.
