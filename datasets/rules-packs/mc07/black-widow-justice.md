# BLACK WIDOW // JUSTICE

Black Widow’s power comes from her years of training, making her ready for any situation. Instead of attacking enemies head-on, she frustrates their plans with cards. Use her Widow’s Bite to stun a minion after it enters play, or use her Grappling Hook to cancel the effects of a treachery card when it is revealed. Each time she triggers the ability of a card, she can deal damage to an enemy with her Widowmaker ability! With the Justice aspect, prevent the villain from advancing their scheme with Counterintelligence, or catch them off-guard with a Stealth Strike. Summon fellow S.H.I.E.L.D. operatives Quake and Agent Coulson to help ensure the villain is brought to justice!
