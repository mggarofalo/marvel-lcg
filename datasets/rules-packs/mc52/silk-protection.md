# SILK // PROTECTION

Silk’s “Silk Sense” allows her to learn her opponent’s tendencies and counter them. This is represented by encounter cards she tucks under her identity using her hero ability. She benefits from these tucked cards when interacting with other cards from the same encounter set (shown in the bottom left corner of the encounter card). Her Spider Claws increase the damage she deals to enemies matching the encounter set of a tucked card, while Outwitted increases her threat removal from matching cards. Silk’s alter-ego ability and cards like Organic Webbing let her spend tucked cards for benefits that can enable her to defeat minions or side schemes to replace the discarded card, allowing her to tuck the cards she wants. With the Protection aspect, Silk can turn her high DEF stat into damage using “Stop Hitting Yourself.” Spider-Man can ready Silk to use additional basic powers, while Scarlet Spider and Energy Shield can keep Spidey around longer.
