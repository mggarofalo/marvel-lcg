# HERCULES HERO PACK

<a id="the-gift-and-labor-decks"></a>
## The Gift and Labor Decks

Hercules Panhellenios, the demigod son of Zeus, is constantly compelled to prove himself either through a personal challenge or as a form of appeasement to another Greek god. To represent these constant herculean tasks, he begins each game with two special 3-card decks. To create the and decks, shuffle the three cards together to form the deck and the three cards to form the deck. Then, place both of these decks facedown in your play area. During the game, while in alter-ego form you may use Hercules’s “New Labors of Hercules” action to reveal the top card of the deck. After a card enters the victory display while in hero form, you may use Hercules’s “Atonement” ability to put the top card of the deck into play. These cards allow you to draw cards on the turn they enter play, minor permanent bonuses, and a benefit to many of Hercules’s hero cards based on how many cards you have in play.

<a id="alternate-player-encounter-card-backs"></a>
## Alternate Player & Encounter Card Backs

This product contains a variation on the standard player and encounter card backs. These card backs allow players to differentiate these cards from other cards of the same type as the rules for these cards prevent them from entering a deck, a discard pile, or a player’s hand.
