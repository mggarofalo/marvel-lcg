# HERCULES // LEADERSHIP

Hercules’s goal is to complete his “herculean” labors, acquire his gifts, and become nearly unstoppable. Although obtaining all three gifts makes him truly godlike, don’t take on more than you can handle. One or two may be all that you need. Note that the word “find” allows you to search all in- and out- of-play areas except facedown encounter cards, the victory display, and any cards that have been removed from the game. Use this to your advantage to find the perfect minion or side scheme for your situation. For example, the Lernean Hydra is always a potential target for Defeat the Hydra.
