# JESSICA JONES // JUSTICE

As a PI, Jessica Jones does a lot of actions in alter-ego form such as Snooping Around or discarding minions with 4K Digital Camcorder. Although she wants to get as many evidence counters as possible on Alias Investigations to defeat a villain stage, she shouldn’t be afraid to spend those counters for other effects such as Calling in Favors. Cards like Breakthrough that can ready Jessica are very useful as each basic power usage gives her more evidence. With the Justice aspect, Jessica wants to complete her player side schemes as quickly as possible since they provide her with evidence counters on top of their normal effects. Captain Marvel, Spider-Woman, and Squirrel Girl are all great allies for Calling in Favors.
