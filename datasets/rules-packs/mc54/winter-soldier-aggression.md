# WINTER SOLDIER // AGGRESSION

Winter Soldier’s Cybernetic Arm is central to his strategy. Use it to pay for and power up any of his many attack events, each of which gain added benefits from using his arm. With the Aggression aspect, load up on weapons like Bambino and the S.H.I.E.L.D. Sidearm, then use Cybernetic Arm to pay for Firepower to deal 4 damage for each weapon you exhaust. Winter Soldier benefits greatly from defeating minions. If the encounter deck is not forthcoming with them, use Safe House #30 or Spoiling for a Fight to find your next target. When you do find a minion, Aggressive Stance can find you the perfect attack event and Man on the Wall will reduce that event’s cost. When you defeat that minion, you can remove threat from the main scheme with Winter Soldier’s “Lethal Protector” ability, then ready up and confuse an enemy with Silent Infiltration.
