# EXPANSION

Unlike previously released expansions for Marvel Champions, the Civil War expansion does not include ﬁve interconnected scenarios and a campaign mode. Instead, this expansion comes with four preconstructed scenarios plus everything you need to build your own custom scenarios. You can use any of the preconstructed scenarios, or your own custom ones, to play a game of Marvel Champions in the traditional cooperative format, or you and a friend can exchange scenarios to challenge each other in the new competitive game mode introduced in this expansion!
