# TIE-BREAK CONDITIONS

In the event of a tie, players can use the following tie- break conditions in order to determine the winner: The team whose enemy main scheme deck did not advance past stage 1B wins. The team with the fewest total minions and side schemes in their game area wins. The team with the least amount of threat on the main scheme in their game area wins. The team whose identities have the most remaining hit points wins. The team with the fewest attachments on the leader in their game area wins.
