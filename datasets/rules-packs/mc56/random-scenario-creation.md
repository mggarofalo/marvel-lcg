# RANDOM SCENARIO CREATION

For players who want to be surprised, or perhaps surprise their opponents, scenarios can be created completely at random: Simply follow the steps of Custom Scenario Creation but choose your side, your leader, your 3-4 modular sets, and your two main scheme stages all at random. If you do this without looking at the modular sets or main schemes, you won’t know all the cards in the encounter deck or what main scheme abilities to expect. This adds a fun layer of tension to each new reveal that keeps the game fresh and exciting.
