# FEATURED TERMS

<a id="find"></a>
## “Find”

When you are instructed to ﬁnd a card, search each area where the card could be found (play area, set-aside area, player deck, discard pile, encounter deck, etc.). Do not search the victory display, cards that have been removed from the game, or facedown encounter cards currently dealt to players. Do not search game areas unnecessarily if you know where the card you are looking for can be found.
