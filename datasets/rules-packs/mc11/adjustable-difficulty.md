# ADJUSTABLE DIFFICULTY

For players who want to experience a greater challenge, The Once and Future Kang comes with an Expert Kang villain set. To play the scenario in expert mode, replace all six villains in the Kang encounter set with the six villains from the Expert Kang set and add the Expert encounter set to the encounter deck. (The Expert encounter set is found in the core set.) This scenario pack comes with three new modular sets: Anachronauts (difficulty 8), Master of Time (difficulty 6), and Temporal (difficulty 4). All three encounter sets can be included in other scenarios, like the modular encounter sets from the core set. For your first time playing The Once and Future Kang, we recommend using the Temporal set. To increase the difficulty of the Kang scenario, you can replace the Temporal set with either the Master of Time set or the Anachronauts set.
