# SETUP

When setting up the Kang scenario, stage 1A instructs the players to set each copy of Kang (II) and Kang (III) aside. This means that Kang (I) is the only villain in the villain deck at the beginning of the game. Kang (II) and Kang (III) will enter play through the card effects on main schemes 3A and 4A. The players must defeat Kang (I), Kang (II), and Kang (III) in order to win the game.
