# SCARLET WITCH // JUSTICE

Scarlet Witch wields powerful chaos magic that often has unforeseen consequences. Her most versatile spell, Hex Bolt, allows for chaotic variety, while Molecular Decay gives her the ability to turn damaging forces against her foes. When the situation becomes too hectic, use her Chaos Control ability and Scarlet Witch’s Crest to shift the odds in your favor, or change to Scarlet Witch’s alter-ego form, Wanda Maximoff, to team-up with her brother, Pietro Maximoff. With the Justice aspect, take down numerous schemes back- to-back through careful Multitasking, Turn the Tide on the villain for extra damage, and call on the aid of both Wiccan and Speed to put away the bad guys for good!
