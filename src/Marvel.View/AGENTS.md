# Visibility-safe meaning

Apply the root and [shared source guidance](../AGENTS.md). Read
[presentation-layer.md](../../docs/presentation-layer.md) before changing this
project. View describes authorized engine facts; it does not decide rules.

## Explain the decision, not just the available controls

Project the current cause, source, affected objects, answering seat, question,
optionality and relevant consequences so consumers can explain the situation
before asking the player to act. Keep resolution context distinct from draft
progress: selecting targets or payment must not erase why the prompt exists.

Preserve stable semantic relationships and exact offered identities. Provide
readable names alongside structured facts; names are display copy, never keys
for inferring action meaning, host relationships, or target roles.

Preserve engine-authored target details and cost distinctions. Describe known
values as known, conditional effects with their conditions, and unresolved
values as unresolved. Project a changed stat or effect from its owning engine
result; do not compute it again here or parse printed text to create a forecast.

Where the engine lacks a fact needed for an understandable interaction, extend
the owning engine contract with tests, then project it. Do not compensate with
a plausible explanation whose accuracy the engine has not established.

## Filter every route to information

Apply the same authorized visibility scope to prompts, board descriptions,
events, action and target summaries, cost choices and explanatory reasons.
Choice counts, absent/present highlights and accessibility copy can leak hidden
facts just as card faces can. Do not project concealed identifiers or ordering
for a client to hide cosmetically.

Expose newly accessible information only as authorized by the engine result
and viewer policy, including ordinary draws into an authorized hand. Searches
and reveals expose only their permitted scope and lifetime. Keep cooperative
and restricted policies explicit. An inspected seat is not evidence of
permission to see its private state or answer its prompt.

## Evidence

Test information retained as well as information removed: sufficient cause,
participants, costs and uncertainty to explain the decision, and no concealed
facts through alternative projections. Cover the affected viewer policies and
seat roles. Follow [tests/AGENTS.md](../../tests/AGENTS.md) for product evidence;
a valid serialized descriptor alone does not demonstrate comprehensible UI.
