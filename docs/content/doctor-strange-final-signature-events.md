simultaneous independent effects. The exact discard receipt tests printed
resource-type presence: R physical stuns, Y energy deals a separate two damage,
B mental confuses, and G wild applies all three. Double icons do not multiply a
branch. Rulings `59bea1238c870653`, `e4ea1bf855e41aac` and
`d1f9f33e3ae4ba2f` agree with current printed/event rules for these specific
facts; their pre-1.5 tags are not authority to restore superseded keyword rules.
Deck reset occurs before the original event's cleanup. Exact enemy incarnation
survives a same-physical-card Norman/Goblin face change, while defeat and villain
promotion do not redirect the later branches to the replacement.

Protective Ward permits another player's treachery reveal and cancels every
effect, including Surge; revelation and discard remain (`rr:cancel.4`). Current
September 14, 2026 ruling `7075a85e02f2203e` distinguishes direct separate-zone
reveals from first-dealt cards. First-dealt cards from any source qualify,
including a scenario-owned card moved to a player deck before dealing. Direct
encounter-discard revelation does not qualify. Return the Favor's distinct
instruction is outside this packet and is not adjudicated here.

Core Enhanced Spider-Sense (01004) and Black Widow (01075) gain only that same
origin qualifier. Their existing form/player constraints, costs and cancellation
programs are retained. Historical replay archives are untouched.
