# Resource-card spend response (MARVEL-882)

The current Rules Reference 1.8 initiating-abilities steps 5 and 6 pay all costs before the card commences being played or its ability initiates. Ruling `46eab153f9f2813b` places a response to a resource card spent between those steps; its historical step numbers refer to an older Rules Reference. Rule `rr:cost.4.1` and ruling `6cb0530e539c7915` distinguish resources paid from those generated in excess: a physical card can be spent even when all its generated resources are excess.

The completed resource receipt retains physical hand contributions, their incarnations and contributing seats, independently of its paid icon string. A single response occurrence owns all cards spent for one completed cost; it precedes entry or the effect and remains inside the initiating execution across generator, cost and response suspensions. An ordinary discard does not create this occurrence. The out-of-play candidate must match the retained physical copy and its owner. Nested payments create their own occurrences.

`SpentResourceResponseTests` uses a test-local 12006 interpretation, including Tiny, Giant and alter-ego form, and does not admit Ant-Man. It covers excess, physical copies, another player's contribution, shared priority, ordinary discard, decline, full-cost ordering and a suspended generator. It also covers effect-choice payment. The canonical ability book remains unchanged. Review and final integration evidence remain separate from this mechanism packet.
