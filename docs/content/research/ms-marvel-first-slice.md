## Behavior authority that determines the first slice

* `05001a` says: “After you play an Attack, Thwart, or Defense event, exhaust Ms. Marvel → return that event to your hand.” [RR event](../../../datasets/rules-reference/entries/event.md) places a resolved event in its **owner's** discard pile, and [RR response.2–4](../../../datasets/rules-reference/entries/response.md) limits each response to once per occurrence and closes the window after all players decline. [RR exhausted.1–2](../../../datasets/rules-reference/entries/exhausted.md) makes the exhaust cost unavailable while she is exhausted. Vendored [ruling `7f69f006cddce07e`](../../../datasets/rulings/rulings.json) says the event is retrieved after full resolution from discard; an event that removed itself from the game cannot be retrieved. [Ruling `556ecad3abe55ce9`](../../../datasets/rulings/rulings.json) says the qualifying words refer to **traits**, not merely `(attack)/(thwart)/(defense)` in ability text. [Rulings `1086649ada076b70` and `b144656def387a1e`](../../../datasets/rulings/rulings.json) confirm off-turn Counter Punch/Wiggle Room recovery and replay within an appropriate open window.
* `05001b` says Teen Spirit discards from the **top of your deck** until a Ms. Marvel card is discarded, then adds **that card** to hand, with a once-per-round limit. [RR player-deck.1, .3, .4](../../../datasets/rules-reference/entries/player-deck.md) governs ordered deck exhaustion, shuffle and facedown encounter card, and explicitly forbids continuing a discard from the new shuffled deck. [RR identity-specific-card.2](../../../datasets/rules-reference/entries/identity-specific-card.md) constrains which cards share the identity set. A precise no-match/empty-deck result must be established from these clauses and the current engine's deck-empty timing before implementation; do not invent a restart or silently loop.
* Later pack mechanics have their own rulings: [Official MarvelCDB FAQ for `05010`](../../../datasets/marvelcdb-faq/faq.json) and [RR event.5](../../../datasets/rules-reference/entries/event.md) apply Embiggen!'s +2 to **each** damage instance, e.g. Melee's two distinct targets; [ruling `ea46606ca85f46ab`](../../../datasets/rulings/rulings.json) distinguishes Shrink's threat *removal* from prevention of placement; [ruling `88369b11361157e4`](../../../datasets/rulings/rulings.json) keeps a printed cap on threat removed. These are later obligations, not grounds to broaden the first slice.

This is the later vertical proof, not an antecedent to the first bounded engine assignment.

**Distinguishing examples for engine and client evidence.**

1. Put two Big Hands copies A/B in known positions. Play A to damage an enemy; after A resolves into its owner's discard, accepting Morphogenetics exhausts Ms. Marvel and moves **A's object id** to her hand, leaving B where it was. Declining leaves A in discard and Ms. Marvel ready. A second attempt for that same event occurrence is unavailable; a later event cannot use Morphogenetics while she remains exhausted. A no-target or canceled event needs the precise [RR event.2–3](../../../datasets/rules-reference/entries/event.md) boundary checked before admission.
2. In a legal defense window, Wiggle Room prevents damage and draws; the response captures that copy before another card/response changes discard order. Return/replay follows the cited ruling. A card removed from game, an event another player played, and an event with only `(attack)` wording but no Attack trait must not appear as eligible recovery. A result/history view identifies the recovered physical card and committed exhaust without leaking unrelated hidden cards.
3. For Teen Spirit in a later slice, arrange nonmatching cards N1/N2 above one Ms. Marvel card M: exactly N1/N2/M are discarded in order, M moves to hand, and the remaining deck stays ordered. With no match before empty, stop before discarding from the new shuffled deck, apply the required reshuffle/encounter consequence once, and leave no fabricated found card. A second Teen Spirit use in the same round is rejected; readiness/form and round reset are separately asserted. These examples are prepared-state rule probes **in addition** to legal public play, not a substitute for it.

Proof for the vertical slice: authority-cited engine tests with positive, decline, wrong-copy, exhausted, off-turn, out-of-game and restart branches; seeded public setup transcript; identical events/digest against uninterrupted continuation; mutation/negative control for wrong event identity and duplicate response; visible prompt/result assertions at View/Client and native Godot if affected.
