# Remaining-content deep research

Tracking: MARVEL-696.

The owner requested implementation research for all remaining content in the
existing 64-pack inventory, including the complete Fear No Evil wave. The earlier
readiness audit is an inventory of gaps, not fulfillment of that request.
Captain America and Wrecking Crew are already implemented and agent-accepted;
Green Goblin and Ms. Marvel have separate deep research packets. This work covers
the other 60 packs, MARVEL-563–622, without restricting depth to the next wave.

Research baseline: `b44eb23b`. Research is not implementation evidence. Each
packet separates verified authority, position-derived or incomplete inventories,
existing engine primitives, unverified compositions, and genuinely new work.
Missing published or vendored sources remain explicit acquisition tasks rather
than guessed card text. Pack codes must be checked against actual source
frontmatter; some codes in the historical readiness audit were offset.

Agents accept ordinary content and engine expansion using authority review,
reasoning, rulings and appropriate tests. Owner judgment is reserved for
materially new UI or a concrete unresolved concern needing a human. A packet
must name such an interaction or concern rather than imposing owner approval on
every pack. Existing correctness and completeness requirements still apply.

prove physical starter composition. The packets identify the provenance and

after complete admission, not permission to expose a partial pack or invent a
factory injection seam. Start dependent mechanics with nonpublic engine fixtures.

## Review, source confidence and remaining acquisition

All 57 locally catalogued packs in this range are represented: an independent
set comparison found every one of their 3,814 joined face IDs in the paired
ledgers. This includes explicitly labelled generic/supplemental faces and is
not a physical-card total. MARVEL-620–622 have preview evidence without complete
joined inventories; the packets do not claim complete authority for them.

- [Independent review](independent-review.md) sampled printed behaviors,
  rulings, capability paths and coverage. Its starter-authority and Jessica
  Jones attribution findings are corrected; sampling is not an exhaustive
  second reading of every card.
- [Original-source review](source-review.md) resolves campaign columns and
  printed box starters using hash-matched PDFs, and identifies Phoenix's missing
  text. Mojo's online PDF was readable but its local download/hash remains
  unverified; that limitation is explicit.
- [Fear No Evil card acquisition research](fear-no-evil-card-acquisition.md)
  goes beyond the scan locator: it inspects 62 of 201 image filenames and maps
  distinctive behavior across villains, schemes, modulars and campaign cards.
  The other 139 scans remain uninspected, and none of the missing scenario text
  is yet joined into executable authority. Current upstream APIs also lack it.

Concrete acquisition work: MARVEL-697 (Fear No Evil encounter corpus), 698
(Phoenix Burning Hunger text), and 699–701 (complete Elektra, Iron Fist and
Shadowland sources). Other hero starters explicitly labelled position-derived
remain candidates until their printed lists are checked; verified campaign-box
lists are identified with source pages. These limits do not prevent the named
independent engine slices, but do prevent claiming whole-pack admission readiness.

MARVEL-630 is complete after the actual additive-content integration in PR439;
its old pending status in historical notes is superseded. Each later pack still
needs its own compatibility evidence. The future UI work is distinct from
content acceptance; current Wrecking Crew review694 has screenshots and focused
questions and awaits agent layout fix695 before approval readiness.
