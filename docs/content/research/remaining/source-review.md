## Sinister Motives: campaign details from mc27

Pages 9, 11, 13, 15 and 17 contain the five campaign setup/victory transitions. At each of scenarios 2–5, the marked reputation nodes' **left Setup** instructions run top to bottom, then the **right Setup** instructions run top to bottom. This ordering is explicit on those pages; it is not the interleaved OCR order. Page 22 is the printed reputation track: numbered checkpoints are 1, 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25; effect boxes join individual nodes. The track's left rewards include a random three-card S.H.I.E.L.D. Tech offer, a mulligan, Aspect Advantage, Enhanced Tech, Planning Ahead, Helicarrier and Symbiote Suit. Its right penalties include three random Osborn Tech selections at separate nodes, main-scheme and side-scheme threat, a minion search per player with facedown encounter fallback, and one facedown encounter card per player. Implement against each **box's joining line**, not merely the printed five-node markers. The reputation conditions on the bottom of p. 22 are victory points in display, no minions, no side schemes, no main-scheme threat, fewer than one-per-player acceleration tokens, and no defeated identities.

Scenario 1 (p. 9) sets Public Outcry to the selected mode, adds Smear Campaign and one random Community Service; expert adds two sand counters and resolves Surging Sands. Victory marks the calculated number of reputation nodes, records Community Service only if in victory display, and records remaining HP in expert. Scenario 2 (p. 11) adds Public Outcry, Smear Campaign and an unrecorded Community Service, then marked reputation Setup boxes; expert restores recorded HP, offers one facedown encounter card per player for a full heal, and places one facedown boost card on each identity. Scenario 3 (p. 13) additionally starts Venom ally under the first player, adds Snitches Get Stitches, offers two encounter cards for an expert full heal, and in expert shuffles the top two encounter cards into each player deck in player order. Its victory counts Illusion cards left in all player decks as Waking Nightmare. Scenario 4 (p. 15) starts Venom ally again, adds Waking Nightmare threat to Light at the End, offers two encounter cards for expert healing, and records the names of **villains in play** at victory as Last Ones Standing. Scenario 5 (p. 17) searches Sinister Assault for minions matching those names and shuffles them in; expert healing costs three facedown encounter cards and places one-per-player extra threat on **each** main scheme. Expert loss here ends the campaign. Its final reputation score is optional after victory.

## Mutant Genesis: campaign details from mc32

The campaign instructions occupy pages 7, 10, 12, 16 and 19; page 24 is the printed log. Scenario 1 chooses distinct roles, puts one random upgrade from each role into play, sets aside a shuffled Future Past deck and reveals Frightened Police. On victory, each Future Past card in the victory display leaves the campaign, while those in encounter deck/discard/play enter the log; remove the starting role upgrades. Scenario 2 shuffles logged Future Past cards into the encounter deck, sets the remaining Future Past deck aside, awards another random role upgrade only if Frightened Police was defeated, reveals Enemy of My Enemy, and records Jubilee and each CAPTIVE ally that entered play on victory. Scenario 3 repeats that Future Past split, can bring logged Jubilee into play and shuffle logged CAPTIVE allies into any player's deck, awards role upgrades only if Enemy of My Enemy was defeated, and reveals Find the Prisoners. Its victory removes from the campaign allies left under Find the Prisoners or Rescue Captives; Jubilee stays logged only if in play. Scenario 4 similarly keys role upgrades to Find the Prisoners, reveals Surprise Attack, and records whether it was defeated; Jubilee is removed from the log if absent at victory. Scenario 5 keys role upgrades to Surprise Attack, reveals Magneto's Fortress and ends the campaign on expert loss. Each scenario 2–5 expert setup can place one acceleration token on the main scheme per player to restore full HP. Winning expert games record remaining HP, capped at base HP by p. 5.

## MojoMania and NeXt Evolution

The published FFG mc39 PDF pp. 9, 13–14, 15, 17–18 confirms MaGog victory records Longshot, checks the single used genre and lets each player record one controlled support/upgrade of cost at most 2 (or 3 if The Champion is Booing Crowd); cost-dash cards are ineligible. Spiral excludes checked genres, can reveal Longshot if he survived, starts each recorded card from any player's deck at aggregate printed-cost threat, and on victory checks **each** used genre. Its new recorded card has cost at most 2, or 3 when main-scheme threat is below ten per player. Mojo may reuse checked genres only after selecting all available unchecked ones, permits **one copy of each** recorded card per player at aggregate-cost threat, and wins the campaign on victory. The mc39 text extraction places Mojo's expert HP/heal instructions after the victory heading; these are setup instructions by the page layout and expert-campaign rule on p. 5. Verify the pinned PDF image before an executable transcription of that final ordering.

The mc40 campaign transitions are on pp. 9, 11, 14, 16 and 18. Morlock Siege records routed villain titles and the number of Morlock allies still in play. On the Run removes only the logged **villain cards** before the scenario setup text, grants one deck search per saved Morlock, and gives every enemy Tough if an earned campaign environment starts in play. Juggernaut starts all earned environments in any order, places one momentum per environment, deals each player a facedown card from a shuffle of Black Tom Cassidy and one-per-player Creeping Willow, and records Hope's damage on victory. Mister Sinister starts earned environments, adds one-per-player threat to Teleported Away per environment, then places the previously recorded Hope damage either on Hope or as threat on Teleported Away. Stryfe similarly adds one-per-player threat per environment to Stryfe's Grasp, chooses Hope damage versus threat, and in player order reveals the first minion or Psionic attachment discarded from the encounter deck before reshuffling the discard pile. Each scenario chooses one new campaign player side scheme; the paired encounter cards for **all choices so far** are shuffled into that scenario's deck (scenario 1 adds its single pair). Expert heal costs vary: On the Run and Mister Sinister use an acceleration token, Juggernaut and Stryfe use one facedown encounter card. Expert loss to Stryfe ends the campaign.
