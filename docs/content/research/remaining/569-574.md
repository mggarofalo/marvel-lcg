## 569 Ant-Man: form identity and conditional effects

Authority: `12001a/b/c`, `12002`–`12010`, `pack:mc12:ant-man-hero-pack` (Foldable Cards, Team-Up, clarifications), `rr:form-change-form`, `rr:flip`, and Hall rulings `ruling:20353696d669e83d`, `ruling:3439b942532c01dd`. The insert permits alter ego to **either** hero face, either hero face to alter ego, and hero to hero. `12005` Resize's ability change does not spend the one voluntary change. `src/Marvel.Rules/State/Forms.cs` deliberately throws `RulesNotImplementedException` for >2 identity faces; `tests/Marvel.Rules.Tests/State/FormsTests.cs` pins that fail-closed behavior. `docs/forms.md` records face index/digest and preservation of counters, damage, status, attachments and exhaustion. Add an offered destination choice, not a binary flip. The chosen face and voluntary-change receipt must persist across save/replay, and hero-form keyword tests must distinguish Tiny from Giant rather than accepting any Hero.

Distinguishing trace: Scott starts exhausted with damage, a status card and a previously used voluntary change. Playing `12005` chooses Giant; physical state remains; `12001c` may respond with one enemy damage; `12008` may heal two; `12009` grants +1 ATK through this turn. It must not also award Tiny `12001a` threat removal/helmet draw, or re-enable another voluntary change. On a fresh turn, voluntary alter ego → Tiny is legal and spends the receipt. `12003` Giant Stomp and `12004` Hive Mind reject the opposite face; `12006` Pym Particles chooses heal vs draw based on the **form at spend resolution**, with a paid-card event receipt. `12020` Swarm Tactics/Team-Up needs both named characters in play and deck identity eligibility. `12011` Hank Pym overpayment needs actual generated resources and counter-based HP; Hall `ruling:dc330186a6c8ca8e` and `ruling:e8c109f4af3b7aac` clarify payment cases. `12025` obligation and Yellowjacket nemesis are separate encounter obligations; no hero admission until all reachable rows pass. Show face, consequence and next commitment in View/Decisions/Godot, with a new form picker and native comprehension evidence if the interface is materially new.

## 570 Wasp: shared forms, split basic powers

Authority: `13001a/b/c`, `13003`–`13010`, `pack:mc13:wasp-hero-pack`, Hall `ruling:0bf3bc9df0ca74ad`, `ruling:f4cbf2c7b6abb136`, `ruling:1b420f298dc7b045`, `ruling:f216f6359fb0bf96`. The form change is the shared 569 prerequisite. Giant `13001c` divides **basic** ATK damage among enemies or basic THW removal among schemes. Each affected enemy is attacked, so each Retaliate fires; Guard prevents assignment to villain. Each chosen scheme is thwarted, but Patrol/Crisis still blocks main-scheme removal even if a side scheme is cleared in that same simultaneous split. That requires an engine-owned allocation/target legality contract and a player-facing draft with totals, roles and order. A generic multi-target event is insufficient evidence for basic-power semantics.

Distinguishing trace: Giant Wasp with ATK 3 distributes 1 to a Guard minion and 2 to another minion with Retaliate; both were attacked, only the latter retaliates, and assigning any amount to villain is rejected while Guard remains. For THW, distribute 1 to a Crisis side scheme and 1 to main: main removes zero even if the side scheme is defeated by this use. Test zero/over-allocation, target replacement, prevention and defeat response `13001a` exactly once per qualifying minion/side scheme. `13003` Giant Help and `13006` Wasp Sting have their own divided event semantics; `13005` Rapid Growth changes form within basic-power initiation and modifies this use. `13007` Pym Particles, `13008` Piercing/Retaliate, `13020` Team-Up, obligation `13026` and Beetle nemesis must be included. `ruling:30744014e3139d48` identifies defeating player as Beetle choice owner. Save an unfinished allocation, with legal targets and cause visible in View/Decisions/Godot.

## 571 Quicksilver: phase receipt and off-turn defense

Authority: `14001a/b`, `14002`–`14011`, obligation/nemesis in inventory, `rr:player-phase`, `rr:basic-power`, `rr:response`; `pack:mc14:quicksilver-protection` gives deck context, not new product procedure. `14001a` readies after **one use** of THW, ATK or DEF per phase, not once per turn and not for event `14003` Always Be Running. Phase must be authoritative across player/villain transitions and save/replay. `14009` Friction Resistance readies after *any* Quicksilver ready, including card effects, and its resource exhaustion is a separate payment event. `14004` Double Time permits two selections of the same option; `14005` max one per phase but its +2 stats last to round end. `14002` Scarlet Witch ally modifies her basic power using discarded encounter boost icons; `14018` Order and Chaos and alter-ego sibling draw require actual in-play Wanda identity/ally and Team-Up admission.

Distinguishing trace: Quicksilver defends another player's villain attack, exhausts, accepts Super Speed and readies; a second basic DEF in that villain phase cannot use the response again. The next player phase resets it even within the same round. Declining first response leaves the per-phase use available for a later basic power; `14003` ready does not consume it. Restoring a save during the attack must not grant a second response. Extend phase receipt state where the engine already tracks limits; inspect `Agenda`/limit keys and server ledger before choosing storage. Client must identify answering seat and optional readiness response.

## 572 Scarlet Witch: encounter-deck boost observations

Authority: `15001a/b`, `15003`–`15009`, obligation/nemesis in inventory, `rr:boost-boost-icon`, `rr:encounter-deck`, Hall `ruling:123a79b5c8900e0c`. `15001a` replaces the icon **count** of a specific encounter card by discarding top encounter card, once per phase; it does not change that card's identity or any other boost ability. `15009` Crest modifies a count by ±1 at the count window. `15004` Hex Bolt first discards all three cards, then resolves applicable branches (`ruling:123a79b5c8900e0c`). `15003` Chaos Magic plays an otherwise legal card while ignoring resource cost, then discards top cards equal to printed cost; `15005` Molecular Decay and `15006` Warp Reality also consume exact top cards. Each exhaustion/shuffle, acceleration token and event order must be deterministic and restartable. The current `AttackBoost`/boost events and generic encounter discard primitives need proof of an interruptible count window and captured replacement icons; no `scw` ability row is admitted.

Distinguishing trace: villain boost card A has two printed icons and a Boost ability; Scarlet Witch chooses Chaos Control, discards B with zero icons, attack strength uses zero from B while A's Boost ability still resolves as printed. A second count in the same phase rejects Chaos Control; a next-phase count permits it. A boost card discarded by replacement remains discarded; an empty encounter deck reshuffles and places exactly the applicable acceleration token, and replay yields the same stream/digest. For Hex Bolt, all three discarded identities and icon counts are captured before assigning damage/threat/draw/status branches. View must describe which icon count was replaced without leaking unrevealed encounter order; Godot needs an intelligible pending count and optional choice if that is new interaction.

**Heroes.** `16001a` Groot prevents incoming damage by removing up to that many growth counters first; `16001b` grows at most two to printed maximum ten; `16006` spends up to four counters for distinct friendly Tough recipients. (4) Nebula I/II or II/III, two schemes, Nebula + Power Stone + Ship Command + Space Pirates + Standard; Technique `Special` resolution and evasion counters alter activation and setup. `ruling:ce1123737f82aa7e` preserves a Technique whose Special could not resolve. ## 574 Star-Lord: risk as cost and secret deck swap

Authority: `17001a/b`, `17003`–`17010`, `pack:mc17:star-lord-leadership`, `rr:deal-deal-an-encounter-card`, `rr:encounter-card`, `rr:cost`, and Hall `ruling:e9c0fff483aa8b09`, `ruling:39c26b6b8d77edfd`. On playing a card **from hand**, hero `17001a` may deal its controller one facedown encounter card as an interrupt payment, reducing that card's cost by three once per round. This is not a resource card, not an immediate reveal, and must remain assigned to the correct seat for later villain phase. Guardian trait applies to Star-Lord's controlled allies while his hero constant is active; it affects same-play interactions such as Knowhere according to `ruling:e9c0fff483aa8b09`. `17001b` setup fetches one actual Element Gun from deck/discard; Smooth Talker swaps a selected hand card with the **unrevealed** top deck card, preserving ownership/order and hidden visibility. `17003` Daring Escape pays a facedown encounter card before ready/draw; `17004`/`17005` scale from current facedown count. `17010` helmet caps **its own** hand-size bonus at +3, not total hand size (`ruling:39c26b6b8d77edfd`).

Distinguishing trace: Star-Lord with no pending cards plays a cost-4 ally from hand and elects discount; he receives exactly one facedown card before payment, pays one remaining resource, later sees one extra encounter reveal. A second hand play in the round cannot repeat the discount. A card played from discard rejects it. If payment is canceled/invalid, no encounter card or cost reduction can leak from a failed transaction. After save/restart, the assigned facedown card reveals exactly once. Smooth Talker with known top card X and selected hand card Y leaves X in hand and Y on top; unauthorized viewers learn neither identity from prompt/preview/history. If deck exhaustion occurs, handle it at the engine deck boundary. The existing encounter-deal, cost and hidden-zone primitives must be bound to this timing and tested; no `stld` ability row or public setup currently proves them. `17024` obligation and five nemesis cards are required for admission.
