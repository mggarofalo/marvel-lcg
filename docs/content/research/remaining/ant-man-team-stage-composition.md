# Ant-Man team-up and stage-bounded status composition

The printed authorities are `datasets/cards/cards.json` faces `12020` and `12032`. These probes use the actual event faces and costs on synthetic research boards. Neither face is authored in the production ability book and the incomplete Ant-Man pack remains outside public admission.

Swarm Tactics requires Team-Up (Ant-Man and Wasp), changes to the other hero form and then readies the hero. On a board with Ant-Man and another player's actual Wasp ally, a test-local `seq` of `changeForm` to `other-hero` followed by `ready` raises `RulesNotImplementedException` during action enumeration before payment, with digest, RNG and agenda unchanged. The same actual face, team and price with a supported draw body is offered, pays and resolves. `AbilityFormDestinationResolver.RequireStandalone` admits the specific other-hero/draw composition, but not other-hero/ready. This is a composition boundary, not evidence that Team-Up or payment itself fails. The exact response ordering, readying and voluntary-change receipt remain unproved for the actual card.

Muster Courage requires the Avenger identity and grants tough to up to the current villain stage number of friendly characters, at most three. The existing `chooseStatusRecipients` fixed maximum cannot directly lower a `printed_stage` expression, but conditional branches comparing that value to three and two select fixed bounds three, two and one. The authored 12032 row uses those branches and a stage-at-least-one guard. The direct dynamic maximum remains an unsupported encoding, not an engine prerequisite for this card.

The actual row on synthetic research boards passes stage I/II/III bounds and zero/one/three recipient selections; other players' alter-ego and Wasp ally are eligible and the villain is excluded. An already-tough character is excluded. Hero form and Avenger identity are required. A `Game.Resolve` duplicate-target rejection preserves prompt, digest, RNG and agenda and accepts a zero-target retry. Synthetic catalog stage zero suppresses the offer and stage four caps it at three; those values are boundary probes, not claims about a printed scenario. Incomplete Ant-Man remains outside public admission.

Against base `aa89da16dbf3d1854be72483ac380da13d794f97`, focused probes and broad `~AbilityData` checks passed 50 tests after a forced non-incremental Release rebuild. A compiled mutant replacing the recipient bound with a constant three failed the stage-I and stage-II assertions. The original source was restored before the rebuild; no engine change remains.
