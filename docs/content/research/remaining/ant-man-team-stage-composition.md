# Ant-Man team-up and stage-bounded status composition

The printed authorities are `datasets/cards/cards.json` faces `12020` and `12032`. These probes use the actual event faces and costs on synthetic research boards. Neither face is authored in the production ability book and the incomplete Ant-Man pack remains outside public admission.

Swarm Tactics requires Team-Up (Ant-Man and Wasp), changes to the other hero form and then readies the hero. On a board with Ant-Man and another player's actual Wasp ally, a test-local `seq` of `changeForm` to `other-hero` followed by `ready` raises `RulesNotImplementedException` during action enumeration before payment, with digest, RNG and agenda unchanged. The same actual face, team and price with a supported draw body is offered, pays and resolves. `AbilityFormDestinationResolver.RequireStandalone` admits the specific other-hero/draw composition, but not other-hero/ready. This is a composition boundary, not evidence that Team-Up or payment itself fails. The exact response ordering, readying and voluntary-change receipt remain unproved for the actual card.

Muster Courage requires the Avenger identity and grants tough to up to the current villain stage number of friendly characters, at most three. `chooseStatusRecipients` supports friendly identities and allies via `heroesAndAllies`, but its `maximum` lowers only a positive integer. A test-local `printed_stage` expression in that field fails lowering. A fixed maximum of three on the same actual cost-three event pays, offers a recipient and gives tough. A fixed three cannot represent stages one and two; no production row is added. Stage-specific limits, trait and form gates, multiplayer, invalid answers, and their retry semantics remain to be proved after a dynamic capped maximum is implemented.

On base `152193322de9d38f624302bcde40934dfddc20c5`, the focused probes and broad `~AbilityData` filter passed 35 tests after a forced non-incremental Release rebuild. A compiled mutant that accepted a map maximum as fixed three failed the Muster probe at the expected exception assertion. The original lowering was restored before that rebuild; no engine change remains. Logs are in the worker's temporary directory (`ant-team-stage-mutant.log`, `ant-team-stage-restored-build.log`, `ant-team-stage-restored-test.log`).
