**Six standalone recipes.** The five interchangeable schemes each choose one of Bullseye, Electro, Hammerhead, Purple Man or Typhoid Mary (two stages Standard I/II or Expert II/III, except Typhoid Mary's two-sided A/B form). Their published fixed/recommended encounter composition follows, with the last two named sets customizable per insert: Protection Racket ×5 main schemes + Disasters + Tracksuit Mafia; The Raft Breakout + The Owl + Tombstone; Art Museum Heist + Cops + The Owl; The Getaway + Cops + Drive; Stop the Presses! + Tombstone + Tracksuit Mafia. Each includes its own scheme/encounter set, chosen villain's set, and Standard (plus Expert in Expert mode). `pack:mc60:protection-racket`, `the-raft-breakout`, `art-museum-heist`, `the-getaway`, `stop-the-presses`. Kingpin uses A1/A2 Standard or B1/B2 Expert, The King's Gambit/Endgame, Kingpin + Tombstone + Tracksuit Mafia, **no Standard set**, and Expert set only in Expert mode (`pack:mc60:kingpin`). Setup must expose a scheme/villain pair, not assume a fixed pairing; invalid duplicate villain or missing companion set must fail. The exact villain/scheme/card IDs cannot be authored until the absent faces are admitted.

**Scenario mechanics.** Protection Racket has one main scheme per player and a swap/ruling that discards attachments from the replaced scheme. Raft Breakout uses Master Key and nemesis sets; Baron Zemo can turn allies with threat into minions, and the ruling says threat stays when they revert. The Getaway has the double-sided Out Front/Alongside attachment and speed counters; `pack:mc60:the-getaway-2` says Out Front's encounter-card replacement removes threat despite Crisis/Patrol. Stop the Presses gives each player a random stamina-based support and reacts when it leaves. Typhoid Mary flips each villain phase; `pack:mc60:typhoid-mary-2` says the newly visible When Revealed/Toughness resolves. Minion defeats accrue Public Support, which flips Kingpin and advances the scheme at the villain-phase start (`pack:mc60:double-sided-villain`). His scenario reveals each player's nemesis minion, resolving title conflict by an alternate encounter minion (`pack:mc60:nemesis-sets`). Before each scenario, two unresolved environments are sampled on the seeded stream (one remaining counts twice); progression checkmarks can fail a scenario at three Xs.
