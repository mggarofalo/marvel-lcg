Shadow King's `36038` Possessed calls for controlled-minion/type and control review, not a generic damage row.

**First slice and controls.** Build the four-card weather setup and a public Storm starter only after divider reconciliation; choose Clear Skies, swap to Thunderstorm, offer its two-damage Special, then save/restart with exactly one active WEATHER support and the other three in the separate deck. Negative controls: missing support, duplicate selection, Blizzard with zero eligible minions, and deck exhaustion/hidden order.
