## Boundary, reuse, and work order

`datasets/setup/setup.json` admits none of these heroes, scenarios, or modular sets, and `datasets/abilities/abilities.json` has no executable rows for them. PR #439 is merged at `6776a8f4` with 11 final checks green; Captain America and Wrecking Crew are real additive admission examples. Generated faces, a synthetic engine test, or a source description is not proof of public play. `SetupCatalogAdmission`, `Dealer`, `DatasetGameFactory`, ability lowering, client selection, and save/replay all need checking at the tested revision of each slice.

Useful existing primitives include `Statuses.Limit` for Steady/Stalwart, `Forms.ChangeAdditional` for extra forms, `PhaseEndTests` for Temporary, `EncounterSetupPlan` and `ActiveVillainSelection` for topology, and RoRS `CampaignProgressRules`/`CampaignReplay` for campaign persistence. Each is a general shape, not a completed card. In particular `Forms.cs` explicitly says additional forms are **not emitted into the state digest**; Shadowcat requires a specified, pinned wire change before admission. The default Tough limit in `Statuses.Limit` is one; Colossus needs an explicit card-derived exception. The controlled-minion-to-ally operation, a two-card identity, campaign roles/Future Past, and Mansion Attack's hidden random villain/scheme queues have no demonstrated product implementation here.

Sequence shared engine work before bulk ability authoring: (1) complete hero physical setup and one public characteristic decision with save/restart; (2) common status, form, attachment, resource provenance and damage-cost semantics; (3) standalone Sabretooth and each successive Mutant Genesis scenario; (4) campaign roles, Future Past and carryover; (5) inventory reconciliation, ordinary regression and native interaction checks. Agents can accept ordinary content/engine work using printed authority, applicable rulings, distinguishing unit/integration evidence, and Godot checks where interaction changes. Reserve owner review for the materially new separated-identity tableau, the Robert Kelly protection decision, or the Mansion Attack hidden rotating villain/location interface if those interactions cannot be established by agent evidence. A later approval item should identify a tested build, native screenshots/video, and focused questions about what players understand; this research provides no such visual evidence.

For tests, cite and quote the exact applicable RR clauses, including `rr:form-change-form`, `rr:status-cards`, `rr:cost`, `rr:enemy-attacks`, `rr:campaign-mode`, `rr:deck-customization`, `rr:setup-keyword`, `rr:temporary`, and the relevant keyword records. Product rule IDs remain separate citations.

Verify the candidate against an original divider before admitting it. The generated pack frontmatter, not an older face-count assertion, fixes this scope.

Spider-Nonsense (`30001a`) gives one toon counter **after any amount of damage taken**, while the alter-ego response (`30001b`) gives one after basic recovery. Tough, prevention and zero damage must not grant a counter; several damage events can grant several. Toon counters are wild payment resources, but `30004`, `30005`, `30010`, `30011` spend them as ability costs; the same counter cannot pay both. `30009` prevents all but one damage, then the actual one damage may trigger Spider-Nonsense. `30005` cancels the effects of a revealed encounter card; preserve the reveal window and no downstream effect. `30006` makes a real villain attack before drawing three. `30002`, `30004`, `30005`, `30009` contain performative printed instructions (high five, read/say/wiggle): determine a consistent engine-owned treatment of their required mechanical costs/effects without inventing an unverifiable gesture gate. `30025` can pull Green Gobbler into play after side-scheme defeat; `30026` clears counters on **each controlled card**, including non-toon counters. Inheritors grant stacked keywords/acceleration and pull minions from an encounter discard operation, so the module needs its own admission and not merely the hero deck.

First slice: public Peter Porker setup → take one preventable damage event → gain exactly one counter → spend it as wild payment → save/restart with identical counter, digest and offers. Negative controls: Tough prevents the damage, two damage in one event gives one counter, insufficient counter fails the cost, nemesis clears counters on upgrades too. View/Decisions should describe the counter source, wild payment and damage consequence; Godot must not calculate legality from icon text. `ruling:a9c2888c8a29122e` (Thwip Thwip! simultaneous resource/damage costs) is relevant to the pack's cross-card payment path. Open question: the official insert has no precise treatment of nonmechanical performance instructions; document a deterministic choice at implementation rather than silently rejecting plays.
