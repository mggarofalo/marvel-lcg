`18001b` permits **up to six attack and/or thwart events from aspects other than the chosen one**. Admission must count copies rather than distinct titles and reject an off-aspect event without either trait; its standard aspect rule otherwise applies. `18001a` has separate once-per-phase Finesse and Precision responses after playing the respective event. A dual-trait event can make both offers; the player chooses whether and how to take the optional responses. `18006`/`18007` check an earlier event **this turn**, a different clock from her once-per-phase limits. `18004` is attack/defense/thwart and combines prevention, damage and threat removal. The vendored `ruling:d9ba3bce596e4d05` on multi-labelled Crosscounter says simultaneous stun/confuse replacement removes one chosen status and the cancelled event is not treated as attacking/thwarting/defending. This requires a discriminating timing test, not keyword checks in Godot.

First slice: extend engine-owned deck assessment (`src/Marvel.Rules/Setup/DeckAspectAssessment.cs`, `PlayerDeckAssessment.cs`) and setup hero admission, prove six legal off-aspect copies versus seven and an illegal non-event, then run one public Gamora setup → attack event → Finesse choice → save/restart. If the event is both attack/thwart, assert both independently offered once in that phase; if a later phase begins the same turn, reset only the phase ledger. UI/View must show each response's source, chosen scheme/enemy and optional status without inferring traits from displayed text. Nemesis Nebula minion `18026` needs its own reveal/attack ownership transcript; do not confuse her with hero Nebula (`22001a/b`) or the GMW villain.

## MARVEL-576 Drax (`drax`, mc19)

`19001a` gains +1 ATK per vengeance counter. **After the villain attacks Drax** it adds one to a maximum of three; at the cap it draws instead. The event is the attacked character, not whether Drax took damage. An ally defending an attack initially against Drax does not trigger this counter: `ruling:34e8c75a6906af4a`. By contrast Payback after an ally defends an attack against his player is allowed (`ruling:3a72e8cc4970e854`). `19001b` removes all counters on changing to alter ego and heals two per removed counter. `19003` readies/draws before causing a villain attack; its attack must go through the normal defense/boost/response sequence. `19006` reads current ATK at its damage-prevention window; `19011` replaces defeat, sets HP to four, changes form and leaves the game.

First slice: an engine attack-target/counter response trace: direct attack on Drax with zero, two and three counters; zero damage; ally defense; enemy attack against another player. Assert only Drax-targeted completed attacks offer the counter, cap draws, and the form flip clears/heals exactly once. Then admit starter and a public setup → villain attack → counter → restart journey. Review response context in View/Decisions: player must see whether Drax, an ally or someone else's character was attacked. The timing of a cancelled attack and of a Drax defeat-replacement nested in an attack deserves a separate ruling-backed slice; do not infer it from a damage trigger.
