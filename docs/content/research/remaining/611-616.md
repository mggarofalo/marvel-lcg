**Topology.** Enchantress starts with random, reverse-hidden Hypnotic Gaze attached to each identity; charm, Enthralled and a forced attachment action create a per-player hidden/revealed lifecycle. The insert FAQ says the action still fires even if its apparent benefit cannot be taken; Future of Despair enters with total 5 per hero Standard or 6 per hero Expert on her advance. Loki HP scales by all players, Worlds Collide per-group icons by all groups across pods. Each group selects its own Standard/Expert difficulty and runs rounds simultaneously without a barrier. Pods partition groups for specific cross-group effects; the organizer chooses pod sizes, with 3–4 groups/12–16 players a recommendation, not a hard cap. Event organizer tracks neutral damage/threat and an optional time limit (default 180 minutes), which must be an explicit externally chosen event input rather than gameplay wall-clock logic. Neutral win/loss is shared. Groups cannot otherwise attack, defend or target another game's area. This reading is from the hash-verified original PDF pages 9–16 and harvested sections.

**First slices and proof.** Start Enchantress with one legal 1-seat Standard recipe; seed and inspect face-down Gaze assignment, reverse-side visibility, charm replacement, forced action, Future of Despair and save/restart. For God of Lies, first prove Single Group neutral-area targeting and four Avatar shared-dial state, then design a server-owned multi-group aggregate with group/pod IDs, atomic neutral receipts, deterministic per-group seeds and replay order, and save of every group's local/neutral state. ## 613 Civil War — combinatorial cooperative and competitive product

**Inventory.** 234 joined faces / 292 summed upstream face-row quantities, including Tigra and Hulkling hero suites (both identities, signatures, obligation and nemesis), four four-stage leaders (Iron Man, Captain Marvel, Captain America, Spider-Woman), their 10-card encounter sets and four leader-specific basic player cards each, eight main-scheme stage-1 choices and eight stage-2 choices across registration/resistance, **eight five-card modular sets per side** (16 total), Standard PvP and additional player cards. See the ledger for every ID and quantity; face-row sums include a/b sides. `pack:mc56:content-overview` and original PDF pp.3–8 establish composition. The story label does **not** define a campaign; the insert defines independent constructed scenarios and two advertised preconstructed scenarios per side. No persistence between games is stated.

**Mode matrix.** Cooperative/solo supports 1–4 seats. Choose registration or resistance; choose a leader on that side; choose 3 **or** 4 same-side modular sets, Standard, one same-side stage 1 and one same-side stage 2. Four prebuilt leader recipes (Iron Man, Captain Marvel, Captain America, Spider-Woman) are advertised. Random creation samples those same choices; RNG must be seeded and choices logged. Cooperative mode reads enemy team as players and says `your leader` cannot resolve; the grim-rule opponent-choice language needs an engine-owned, deterministic offer/choice policy, not arbitrary host automation. Cooperative customization may include other-product modulars as described on original PDF p.5; competitive cannot include neither-side modulars. Competitive is **only 1v1 or 2v2**, registration versus resistance. Each team independently constructs one scenario, sets aside its leader's four basic player cards, and trades scenarios so each faces the opposing leader, scheme and encounter deck. Standard PvP replaces Standard. Team areas have separate state/visibility. Original PDF pp.14–16 resolves the interleaved harvested text: every round registration hero phase, resistance hero phase, registration villain phase, resistance villain phase; cards in the opposing game area are out of play locally unless explicit. “Your leader” is in the opponents' area, “enemy leader” locally. The first player on the attacking leader's team supplies boost and resolves an attack against their own leader. A Stage II leader revealed in villain phase gives each player on the attacked side a facedown encounter card and cannot take more damage until end of that villain phase. Three wins are enemy leader defeated, your leader's scheme 2B completed, or opposing identities all defeated, with equal-phase completion; the insert has ordered tie breakers (`pack:mc56:tie-break-conditions`).

**Dependencies and first slices.** Existing Leader kind and conventional villain/damage primitives are useful, but setup composition, team ownership, reciprocal scenario trading, cross-area targeting, set-aside earned player cards, round coordinator and win/tie contract are unverified or missing. First exercise a nonpublic engine fixture for one cooperative 1-seat Iron Man Standard prebuilt scenario, including exact stage IDs, selected modular sets and hero setup; negative control swaps one resistance module or wrong-side scheme. Then add 1v1 as a separate capability behind a pair-of-areas authoritative session; do not infer support from the cooperative slice. Prove a registration win followed by the resistance catch-up phase, a simultaneous victory tie and each ordered breaker, save/restart between the two teams' phases, hidden opposing encounter order, and attacks on your own leader. Tigra `56010`–`56022` and Hulkling `56041`–`56053` each sum to 25 position-derived starter additions; reconcile with original p.18 before setup authoring. Competitive two-table/team navigation, role and known-consequence clarity are a specific owner UI review surface after working evidence. No generic owner signoff is needed for ordinary heroes, leader logic or cards.
