## Authority and assessment

The generated card text specifies 1 damage to each minion followed by 8 to an enemy. RR 1.8 `rr:attack-player-ability-type.2.1` describes one attack despite multiple damage instances; `.5` treats each targeted enemy as attacked. `rr:retaliate-x.1` triggers after being attacked and `.2` requires the character still to be in play. `rr:prevent.1.3` distinguishes an attack whose damage is entirely prevented from having attacked and damaged the target. `rr:leaves-play.1` makes a returning card a new copy.

No blocking defect was established in this bounded recipient repair. A fixed-target attack with zero or two minions reaches the expected damage and enemy response counts; a surviving repeated minion has one response, one retaliation and nine damage recorded. The rules-level accumulation test separates incarnations. Review of the relevant eligibility code confirms a departed minion cannot retaliate, and retaliation rechecks the attacker's incarnation and play area; this is code review, not new end-to-end evidence for departure or terminal attackers. Existing staged-attack edge and receipt tests exercise cancellation, ordering, Guard, damage prevention and Melee; they do not prove the unimplemented Giant Stomp choice. Capturing first-recipient facts when aggregating repeat damage is not independently tested against changes to snapshot facts during the attack.

New independent probes protect either the splash minion or the later villain with Tough, then assert zero recipient damage, an enemy attack response and one retaliation. These probes do not independently exercise all-prevented damage-trigger admission, cancelled damage, re-entry during this particular composition, or terminal attacker cleanup.
