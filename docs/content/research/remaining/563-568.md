## MARVEL-563 Thor — hero

**Scope.** Identity `06001a/b`; fifteen signature copies `06002–06010`; position-derived starter candidate `06002–06025` (signature 15 plus Aggression/Basic 25). Obligation `06026` Odin's Anger; nemesis `06027–06030` Family Feud, Loki, Frost Giant ×2, Trickster (five nemesis copies). `06031–06034` are twelve extra customization copies, outside the starter candidate. Each face and quantity is in the inventory. `pack:mc06:thor-hero-pack` describes divider ordering; `pack:mc06:thor-aggression` confirms the intended Aggression combination. The divider list itself is not vendored, so pack positions alone do not verify the starter.

**Mechanics and authority.** Thor `06001a` draws two after **engaging** a minion, once per phase; `rr:engage` and clauses `.2–.3` distinguish entering/re-engaging from merely revealing and prohibit re-engaging an already engaged minion. `06003` discards encounter cards until a minion, then puts it into play engaged with Thor before removing threat. `06014` can move another player's minion only with Aerial, and the vendored MarvelCDB FAQ for `06014` explicitly rejects re-engaging one's current minion. Odinson `06001b` and For Asgard `06004` search deck **and discard**, choose a match, and shuffle after deck search (`rr:search.1–.3`). `06005` exhausts Mjolnir as a cost, deals eight with overkill, and returns Mjolnir to hand; inspect event payment and departure before treating the returned upgrade as still granting ATK/Aerial. `06006` Lightning Strike is *not* an attack event: FAQ `06006` corrects its text to “This damage ignores…”; the generated text carries the corrected “damage” wording. FAQ `06028` makes Loki's defeat prevention a **Forced Interrupt**. `rr:limit` says per-phase use is per ability instance, including canceled initiation.

**Existing versus needed.** `CardEntryDestination`, `BasicPowerStatus.Engaged`, and the ordinary encounter reveal path model engagement; `docs/card-dsl.md` already names discard-until, engagement selectors, physical costs and return-to-hand. Prove their composition first in bounded nonpublic engine/content tests; no current Thor ability validates it. Complete-pack admission follows after the full inventory and setup are authored. First engine/content slice: Odinson search plus Mjolnir and Thor response, using an encounter minion entering play and `Get Over Here!` as separate engagement causes. Distinguishing example: reveal a minion to player A while Thor is B (Thor draws none); Aerial Thor then engages that minion from A (two cards); a second attempted engagement with the same Thor must be unavailable, and a later engagement during the same phase must not draw again. An encounter discard from Defender must only draw if it actually enters engaged. Save/restart must retain Mjolnir location, per-phase use, and the encounter discard order. The offer must explain the chosen search zone and when a card is returned to hand.

**Open bounded check.** Compare physical Thor divider to the position-derived forty; if it differs, correct setup evidence before authoring. Check any timing ambiguity against the Ronan/Thor rulings in the vendored FAQ and the rules graph before finalizing tests.

## MARVEL-564 Black Widow — hero

**Scope.** Identity `08001a/b`; signature fifteen copies `08002–08010`; position-derived starter `08002–08024` (Justice/Basic 25). Obligation `08025` Burn Notice; nemesis `08026–08029` Taskmaster, Killer for Hire, Hydra Mercenary ×2, Deadly Shot. Extra customization copies `08030–08033` total twelve. Rulesheet `pack:mc07:black-widow-justice` names Widow's Bite, Grappling Hook, Counterintelligence and Agent Coulson but does not list the divider contents.

**Mechanics and authority.** `08010` Widow's Bite is a response *after a minion enters play* and discards itself to deal two and stun; `08008` Grappling Hook interrupts **reveal of a treachery**, canceling effects and discarding it. `08006` Attacrobatics interrupts a boost card turning faceup, cancels its icons and deals damage; `08024` Target Acquired responds after the same flip but cancels the boost **ability** instead. Their different windows and canceled parts must remain distinct. `08017` Counterintelligence prevents threat before placement. `08018` Spycraft cancels a revealed encounter card; `08033` Espionage interrupts surge resolution. Widowmaker `08001a` deals one after the player triggers an ability of a Preparation **they control**; Natasha `08001b` draws after playing a Preparation, once per phase. `rr:timing`, `rr:forced`, and `rr:limit` govern ordering/limits; source trait/control and a canceled effect are the distinguishing facts. The vendored card FAQ has no direct entries for these signature Preparation faces; `08012` does clarify that a minion scheming for zero still schemed, so Quake may respond.

**Existing versus needed.** The timing agenda already has pre- and post-occurrence windows, cancelability and source-controlled abilities (`docs/timing.md`); engine event payment and `rr:limit` machinery should be reused. There are no Black Widow ability rows to prove nested trigger ordering. First slice: one Widow's Bite entry response with Widowmaker and Synth-Suit `08009`, followed by Grappling Hook at treachery reveal as a second window. Example: a minion enters play, Bite fires, then Widowmaker can hit a distinct enemy and Synth-Suit may ready Widow; the minion's own When Revealed cannot be treated as a second entry. For a treachery, Hook cancels that treachery, not the already resolved card-reveal fact; compare Spycraft and surge later. Persist which physical Preparation was discarded and per-phase Mission Prep use. The View/Decisions prompt must expose the source, exact trigger window, and optional follow-up responses without guessing from the printed word “Preparation.”

**Open bounded check.** Confirm with local timing tests whether an ability whose effect was canceled still counts as “triggered” for Widowmaker; `rr:limit.1` answers limit consumption, but it does not by itself answer this response condition. Do not infer it from a generic cancel implementation.

## MARVEL-565 Doctor Strange — hero

**Scope.** Identity `09001a/b`; signature fifteen copies `09002–09011`; position-derived Protection/Basic starter `09002–09026` (40). Obligation `09027` Physical Toll; nemesis `09028–09031` Baron Mordo, Open the Dark Dimension, Counterspell ×2, Thoughtcasting. Five **one-copy Invocation** faces `09032–09036` form their own deck/discard, never the player's 40-card deck. Extra customization `09037–09039` totals seven face copies. `pack:mc08:protection` describes the starter, but the mc08 local rules snapshot omitted the dedicated Invocation section. The [official FFG mc08 rulesheet](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/6c/40/6c4068b6-00ee-4060-9855-335b826de33d/mc08_doctor_strange_rulesheet.pdf) supplies it: shuffle all five Invocations at setup, keep the top faceup, resolve it into a separate discard pile, and when the deck is empty shuffle that discard back with no penalty. This source must be pinned locally before execution; the facts themselves are no longer unknown.

**Mechanics and authority.** Doctor Strange `09001a` exhausts and pays the *top Invocation's* cost to resolve its Special; `09005` Master of the Mystic Arts resolves that Special then places the same card back on top, unlike ordinary Invocation text that sends it to its separate discard. Stephen `09001b` and Wong `09002` discard the top Invocation to cycle. `09029` Open the Dark Dimension parks that physical top Invocation facedown beneath a scheme, then shuffles it back upon defeat. `09035` Vapors of Valtorr replaces one status with a different status; FAQ `09035` says one cannot replace Tough with a Stunned/Confused status an enemy already has. `09014` Iron Fist has FAQ errata changing Response to Interrupt. General `rr:search`, `rr:resource`, `rr:status-cards` and the printed Special instructions are inputs; the Invocation lifecycle needs its insert as a product authority.

**Existing versus needed.** `AuxiliaryDeck` supports only `ExperimentalWeapons` and `SideSchemes` (`AuxiliaryDeckKind.cs`), with different reset rules. It does not currently express an Invocation deck. `docs/card-dsl.md` has `initializeAuxiliaryDeck` for those two only. First shared engine slice: a typed Invocation draw/discard/reshuffle lifecycle with five setup cards, top-card-only action/payment, and safe disclosure of the visible top card; then author Doctor Strange and Master of the Mystic Arts against it. Example: resolving Winds of Watoomb normally draws three then sends it to Invocation discard; Master of the Mystic Arts resolves the same Special but leaves that same copy on top. Open the Dark Dimension removes the exact top physical Invocation until defeated. Save/restart must preserve order, top visibility, discard and hosted facedown identity without leaking hidden future draws.

**Open bounded check.** Vendor the official mc08 Invocation section through the offline rules-pack provenance process and verify the divider list. The missing local section is an acquisition gap, not an unresolved rule interpretation. Other signature events can be researched meanwhile.

## MARVEL-566 Hulk — hero

**Scope.** Identity `10001a/b`; signature fifteen copies `10002–10010`; position-derived Aggression/Basic starter `10002–10024` (40). Obligation `10025` Inner Demons; nemesis `10026–10028` Abomination, Total Destruction, Clash of the Titans ×3. Extra customization `10029–10032` totals twelve copies. `pack:mc09:hulk-hero-pack` supplies the divider convention; the exact divider decklist remains to be checked.

**Mechanics and authority.** Hulk `10001a` has **Forced Interrupt: when your turn ends, discard your hand**. The engine already defines `WhenPlayerTurnEnds` before turn advance (`docs/card-dsl.md`), so the event exists but Hulk's entire-hand forced selection/commitment must be proved. `10002` Crushing Blow permits **only physical resources**; `10003` Hulk Smash, `10004` Sub-Orbital Leap, `10006` Unstoppable Force, and `10014` Drop Kick grant their enhancement only if paid using physical resources alone. `rr:resource.2–.4` permits wild to act as physical when declared that way; payment evidence must retain the actual types selected, not just price. `10007` Limitless Strength can be spent only in hero form. `10015` Toe to Toe's FAQ explicitly says the five damage still occurs when a stunned enemy's attack is canceled by its status; the attack initiation was not an arrow cost. `10028` chooses the highest-ATK enemy and hero/ally with ties decided by first player, a separate nemesis slice.

**Existing versus needed.** End-turn occurrence and `discardHandCards` are documented engine mechanisms, as are typed/repeated resource contracts in `docs/affordances.md`; no Hulk row proves full-hand forced discard or “only physical” enhancement receipts. First slice: Enraged and one payment-sensitive signature (Sub-Orbital Leap). Example: Hulk ends his turn with two cards after an optional end-turn response: both are discarded before the next player acts; a no-card hand causes no selection. Paying Leap with one printed physical plus a wild declared physical earns five threat removal, whereas a mental payment yields three (assuming both legal); crushing Blow rejects a mental payment outright. The client must state required resource type or conditional enhancement before payment. Save/restart and undo preserve payment choice and turn-bound trigger without a second discard.

**Open bounded check.** Verify the exact end-turn forced-window ordering against other end-turn responses and the current occurrence tests, then assert a distinguishing transcript. Do not treat the ability's printed “when your turn ends” as after turn advancement.

## MARVEL-567 Ronan Kree Fanatic — modular set

**Scope and source.** Generated `ron` faces `90001–90005`, all in `kree_fanatic`: Ronan the Accuser ×1, Judge, Jury, Executioner ×1, The Accused ×1, Bring the Hammer Down ×2, You Dare Oppose Me? ×2. Unlike a scenario, it supplies no villain stages/main scheme or separate starter. The local rules-pack corpus has no `ron` insert. FFG's [official print-and-play PDF](https://images-cdn.fantasyflightgames.com/filer_public/af/e7/afe79820-8835-4503-b256-7208e425d769/ronan_modular_set.pdf) prints the same seven numbered cards, and [FFG's product page](https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/products/marvel-champions-the-card-game/) lists “Ronan Modular Set” under Print-and-Play Cards. This establishes the primary source and quantities but has not been vendored/pinned here; do so before relying on it offline. A modular recipe may insert it into a scenario that permits a modular set; no scenario is bundled with it. Do not confuse `90001` with the Galaxy's Most Wanted villain `16103–16105`.

**Mechanics.** `90001` has Toughness, cannot be stunned, and at villain-phase start engages the hero with the fewest remaining HP; its boost puts it into play engaged with the recipient. `90002` adds threat after an enemy attack defeats a friendly character; its boost enters the side scheme. `90003` attaches to identity, gives an attacking enemy +1 ATK, and remains until Ronan is defeated. `90004` makes Ronan activate against the player he is engaged with, or gains surge if absent. `90005` discards five encounter cards, dealing each Kree Fanatic card as an encounter card to the resolver. `rr:engage`, `rr:deal-deal-an-encounter-card`, `rr:surge`, `rr:timing`, and the exact card text govern. The vendored MarvelCDB FAQ has no entries for `90001–90005` (the queried list includes the codes), so no local card ruling resolves tie timing.

**Existing versus needed.** Ordinary modular composition exists in authored `encounter_sets`, and minion engagement areas already exist. Whole-product admission, a Kree Fanatic encounter-set row and all five ability rows are absent. First slice: insert the seven physical copies into one existing modular-slot recipe through public setup, then exercise Ronan's phase-start re-engagement and boost entry. Example: Ronan engaged with A when phase begins, B has fewer remaining HP, so he moves to B and B becomes the next attack recipient; an exact HP tie needs the published tie rule or first-player decision if authority delegates it. Persistence must retain minion engagement and facedown dealt cards. UI explains that Ronan's forced move is a minion engagement, not a new villain target.

**Open bounded check.** Inspect official PDF metadata/printable copy, pin it under the provenance policy, and settle HP-tie choice by Rules Reference/official ruling before authoring a deterministic choice. Ordinary allowed modular slots can proceed independently of any campaign using this set.

## MARVEL-568 The Once and Future Kang — scenario

**Scope.** 58 generated faces, 80 summed face quantities. `kang` has 36 faces: six Standard villains (`11001–11006`), seven double-sided main schemes (`11007a/b–11013a/b`), and sixteen other encounter face IDs `11014–11029` with printed quantities (including Kang's Dominion ×4 and four two-copy obligations). `exp_kang` supplies six replacement villains `11034–11039`. Three modular sets are `temporal` (`11030–11033`, seven physical copies), `anachronauts` (`11040–11046`, nine), and `mot` Master of Time (`11047–11051`, eight). The [inventory](563-568-inventory.md) resolves every count. `pack:mc11:adjustable-difficulty` directs Expert to replace **all six** Standard villains with six Expert versions and add the Core Expert set; it recommends Temporal and permits substituting Master of Time or Anachronauts. `11007a` names Standard, Kang, one modular set and set-aside Kang II/III/Kang's Dominion. There is no campaign deck.

**Scenario procedure and authority.** `pack:mc11:setup` says only Kang I starts in villain deck; Kang II and III enter from main schemes 3A and 4A. `11001` defeats into stage 2 **at phase end**. `11008a` discards side schemes, adds their count as acceleration to central stage 2, and each player reveals a **random** distinct 3A in turn order; unused stages leave the game. `pack:mc11:areas` says each 3A creates its own game area and Kang II, deals its player one encounter card, then flips 2A to 2B only after all 3A reveals finish. `pack:mc11:game-areas` prohibits cross-area attacks, defense and targets but keeps one shared encounter deck/discard and normal player/first-player order. `11008b` is centrally active outside every other game area and blocks reunion until no other area remains. On Kang II defeat, its scheme leaves and the player joins another area at phase end; if a 3B scheme completes first, a facedown Kang's Dominion goes under 4A, its Kang II and scheme leave, and that area joins another. Stage 4 reveals Kang III and each accumulated Dominion; 4B searches each player's nemesis minion. `pack:mc11:rules-clarifications` limits “each player” to the current area, allows duplicate uniques while separated but resolves them on joining, and says Kang statuses/attachments do not transfer to a later villain. `pack:mc11:new-keywords` defines Incite, Piercing and Villainous.

**Existing versus needed.** `src/Marvel.Rules/State/GameArea.cs`, `WorldTopology.cs` and `Places.cs` already implement multi-area membership, reachability, central cards and joining. `tests/Marvel.Rules.Tests/State/PlacesTests.*` exercise Kang-shaped split/rejoin and uniqueness; `docs/places.md` explains the existing topology. This is a genuine reusable prerequisite, not a missing generic area model. The absent part is a Kang scenario recipe/ability book and the stage-flow orchestration that creates areas, assigns physical Kang II variants and rejoins at **phase end**. It also needs View/Decisions/Protocol/Godot presentation of each player's own villain and scheme, a central 2B, valid local targets and group joining. First implementation slice: public Standard setup with Temporal, then deterministic two-player defeat of Kang I into two distinct 3A reveals and isolated turn actions, save/restart, and phase-end reunion. Only then add 3B completion, stage 4, Expert replacement and other modules.

**Distinguishing examples.** In two areas, a player cannot defend the other area's Kang II or attack its minion; both draw from the same encounter deck. A central 2B forced effect reaches both; “each player” on a local scheme reaches only its area's members. When one Kang II dies mid-phase, that player's area remains isolated until phase end; its engaged minions and side schemes follow the join, and duplicate unique cards are reconciled. A Kang II attachment/status never carries to Kang III. The public board must make the answering seat, current area, local villain, central scheme and pending join explicit. This split tableau is a materially new UI interaction requiring human acceptance after agent/native proof.

**Open bounded check.** Inspect all `11007–11013` front/back attributes and the mc11 insert against an authored setup model before deciding whether the existing recipe shape can represent random, seat-owned 3A stages. Verify first-player tie/unique discard choice and stage 3 completion order in a narrow transcript; do not assume the generic topology tests implement the scenario. Keep `11018–11021` obligations and the alternate modular sets in the whole-pack admission inventory even if the first slice uses Temporal.
