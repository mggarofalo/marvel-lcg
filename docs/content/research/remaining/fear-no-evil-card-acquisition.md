## Campaign face observations

All 12 sides `60205a/b`–`60210a/b` were read from the gallery. The first five are **outcome-dependent campaign environments**; each side changes later setup, so save/restart must keep the physical card's side and the scenario result. The [Art Museum pair](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/60205a.jpg) gives identities tough after success, while failure makes each player choose stun or confuse (both in Expert). [Getaway success](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/60206a.jpg), [Protection Racket success](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/60207a.jpg), and [Raft success](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/60208a.jpg) permit post-mulligan searches for upgrade/support/ally respectively, with a discard after taking one in Expert; their failed sides respectively reveal an encounter attachment (or deal a card if none), discard hand cards, and return Raft minions to the encounter deck (with extra facedown dealing in Expert). [Stop the Presses success](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/60209a.jpg) gives each player an unremoved Daily Bugle support; [failure](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/60209b.jpg) deals facedown encounters (two in Expert). [Campaign Typhoid Mary/Bloody Mary `60210`](https://hallofheroeslcg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/60210a.jpg) is a two-sided ally: its Mary face flips after a minion reveal and its Bloody face after a side-scheme reveal, clearing damage on each flip. Test state through reveal windows, control, and later campaign setup. The rulebook's campaign timing/selection remains authoritative for when these faces enter play.
