## Ordered campaign transition

1. **Campaign identity and scenario choice** (PDF pp. 5, 8–9, 28). Keep each selected identity for the campaign; decks/aspects may change between scenarios under normal deck construction. The five interchangeable schemes are Art Museum Heist, The Getaway, Protection Racket, The Raft Breakout and Stop the Presses!; each combines a scheme with an UNDERLING villain. The players choose the next unresolved scheme as a group after progression, not necessarily one just drawn. When all five are completed or failed, Kingpin is mandatory. A lost game does not automatically fail its scenario. The log stores player identities and HP, each scenario's completed box, villain and three progression boxes, removed titles under the exact heading **Allies and Persona Supports Removed from the Campaign**, and Trust/Mary boxes.
2. **Before every game, including the first and retries** (p. 9 steps 1–4; p. 8). Gather environments 205–209; exclude completed and failed scenarios; shuffle and draw two remaining at random, or the sole remaining one. Progress each drawn scenario by marking its first unmarked box; if only one was drawn mark two boxes. Three marks mean failed. Then select any still-unresolved scenario; if none remain choose Kingpin and skip villain selection. Random ordering and selections require durable, replayable engine authority; algorithm/seed allocation is an implementation choice, not printed authority.
3. **Villain assignment** (pp. 8–9 steps 5–6). Reuse a villain already recorded for the chosen scheme. Otherwise, on first campaign playthrough players may choose the first unrecorded villain in the recommended sequence Hammerhead, Bullseye, Electro, then Purple Man or Typhoid Mary; otherwise choose an unrecorded UNDERLING randomly. Record the chosen villain. The final line offering Purple Man or Typhoid Mary is a choice, not a deterministic tie-break. Gather that villain, scheme and their encounter sets. First-playthrough status must be explicit; the book does not prescribe how software infers it.
4. **Setup** (p. 9 steps 7–14). Proceed with scenario setup until player setup. Each player searches their deck for titles recorded as removed and removes them from the game, adding legal cards if below minimum deck size. Proceed with player setup. In expert campaign set HP from the previous game’s record, then each player may deal themselves one facedown encounter card to heal damage equal to REC. After each game record remaining HP, capped at the identity’s base HP. An expert-campaign player defeated while teammates win does not participate in that scenario’s Victory steps and may rejoin during the next setup using the printed healing instructions (p. 9). Put each completed/failed environment into play on the matching side and resolve its Setup abilities. If Trust Established is checked and Mary Defeated is not, put 210A into play under any player's control. Finally execute the chosen scenario's additional campaign setup. These are ordered instructions; after-mulligan triggers on 206A–208A must retain their printed timing.
5. **Underling outcome** (pp. 13, 15, 17, 19, 21). On victory, in order: if Disturbed Psyche 112 is in play with at least 2 threat check Trust Established; if ally 210 is in the victory display check Mary Defeated; record titles of each unique ally and support removed from the game; check that scenario Completed; return to campaign setup. On defeat, expert campaign progresses that scenario once, then all campaigns return to setup. The first new setup can progress it again. Keep the distinction between a game defeat and a third progress mark that fails a scenario. General `rr:modes-of-play.10` applies to cards actually removed from the campaign, including retries; the victory instruction records unique ally/support titles removed from the game. Whether a particular card effect removes from the campaign must be established from that effect, not inferred merely from leaving play.
6. **Kingpin outcome** (p. 23). At setup, if at least three Completed environments are in play, give each minion in play tough; at least four, find and reveal James Wesley. On victory the campaign is won. On defeat the page says “Standard Mode Only: Replay the scenario” and “Expert Campaign Only”: if a Completed environment remains, choose and flip one to Failed and replay; otherwise lose the campaign. The different qualifiers and independent scenario/campaign modes leave a material overlap/omission question; see unresolved questions rather than choose precedence. A flip must persist as a failed outcome on subsequent setup, not only be a transient game-card flip; the exact log update representation is an engine choice.

Do not duplicate or restore removed supports, deal substitute encounter cards or invent replacements. Ordinary discard or removal of an encounter attachment does not automatically ban it from the campaign; apply each effect’s wording.

**Mary** (PDF pp. 5, 9, 13/15/17/19/21; scans `60210a.jpg`, `60210b.jpg`). 210A is the unique Typhoid Mary ally, 210B the Bloody Mary ally, both Mary Walker, 3 HP and Victory −1. A forced response after its controller reveals a minion clears its damage and flips to B; B does likewise after its controller reveals a side scheme and flips to A. A has Setup; B does not. Apply the printed forced responses and side-specific stats; do not confuse this ally with the separately numbered villain 110/111. If defeated, `rr:victory-x.1.1` and `.2` place the character in the shared out-of-play victory display (`rr:victory-display`); merely leaving play by another means does not establish defeat. The underling victory step checks presence of either 210 face in that display and then prevents future setup through Mary Defeated. Victory −1 is a printed value; no FNE campaign scoring effect is stated on the inspected pages. Do not infer that it undoes a completed scenario. On p. 5 the Typhoid Mary villain's separate encounter setup places Establish Trust 113A and Disturbed Psyche 112 and uses a randomly selected villain side; its victory condition and trust result must be implemented from its card and scenario authorities.

## Question that must not be guessed

**Kingpin defeat difficulty:** p. 23 says **Standard Mode Only** replay and **Expert Campaign Only** flip one Completed environment and replay, or lose if none remains. `rr:modes-of-play.11–12` and the RR campaign FAQ permit scenario and campaign difficulties independently:

| Scenario mode | Campaign mode | Literal p. 23 coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Standard | Replay. |
| Expert | Expert | Flip one Completed environment and replay, or lose if none remains. |
| Standard | Expert | Both apply. With a Completed environment, flip and replay agree; without one, unconditional replay conflicts with campaign loss. |
| Expert | Standard | Neither qualified instruction supplies continuation. |

The [independent review](fne-campaign-rules-independent-review.md) searched local rulings and MarvelCDB FAQ, the Hall of Heroes FFG rulings page and targeted qualifier/errata queries without finding an applicable answer; the FFG support redirect and follow-up fetch failed. This bounded negative search does not prove no answer exists. Seek authoritative clarification for the two unresolved combinations; preserve the printed labels and fail explicitly on unsupported cases until resolved. Do not rewrite Standard Mode as Standard Campaign or force expert scenario mode from expert campaign. Other progression work can proceed independently.

A Kingpin flip must persist as a Failed outcome; replacing the completed checkbox or using a separate override is an engine representation choice. Never reconstruct both sides simultaneously.
