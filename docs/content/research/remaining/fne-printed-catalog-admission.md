## Editorial mapping

The printed facts are the accepted candidate fields; catalog IDs, slugs, attribute keys and serialization are repository choices. Candidate title/subtitle, traits, paragraphs, punctuation and HTML bold/italic emphasis are retained. Only `[per_player]` becomes the existing `[per_hero]` token and `[cost_arrow]` becomes `→`. Product pictograms keep their accepted transcription tokens. Generated `text_plain` comes from the existing extractor; candidate-derived plain text is not imported as a competing field. Flavor, credits, copyright, printed numbers and positions remain source evidence rather than gameplay text.

Separate `boost_ability` fields are appended after `<hr>`, with their printed star and bold Boost label; fields already containing that label retain it. Full-text candidates already contain their boost section. Numeric Boost values and reminder stars are independent; star-only boost rows keep numeric zero and a special-star annotation. True printed uniqueness fields populate `Unique` even when the candidate attribute dictionary omits it (including the Owl faces).

Attachment modifiers use the established `ATK+`, `SCH+`, `THW+` keys. Cold as Ice and Hard as Marble therefore map candidate `SCH: +2` / `ATK: +2` and their stars to modifier keys. The four ART attachments retain both SCH+ and THW+ from their shared badge; Black Cat retains both SCH and THW from hers. Per-player values use `*`; consequential damage appends one `*` per glyph only to ally ATK/THW. Reminder stars stay in `stat_annotations`. Shop Proprietor has two consequential damage each; campaign Mary allies have one each. A dash cost stays an annotated `—` with no invented numeric cost.

Cop's standalone SCH star becomes annotation value `★`, without a numeric SCH attribute. Other standalone star boxes likewise have no invented number. Museum escalation X uses the existing numeric variable base `0*` with `value: X`, per-player true and its independent special star. Jackknifed Tanker Truck's candidate annotation key `Threat` maps to `StartingThreat`. Keyword attributes come from printed keyword lines and exact Uses/Teamwork arguments; conditional grants stay text. Hinder per-player glyphs and campaign Victory −1 are retained as `Hinder: n*` and `Victory: -1` respectively. New expansions remain outside executable admission.

Numeric scheme stages `1A`/`1B` share Stage `1`, following the existing extractor. Kingpin's A1/A2/B1/B2 stage markers stay literal; Typhoid Mary's printed A/B markers stay literal. Observed gameplay reverse associations remain in the inventory below. The existing full-card schema has no independent physical reverse-pair field; `linked_to` means the Linked keyword, so no arbitrary reverse attribute or Linked relationship is invented.
