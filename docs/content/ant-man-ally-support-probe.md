# Ant-Man ally and support composition probe

Baseline: `3f622aee8a69e29d3325a3a09c804d08caa00ac9`. Research-only synthetic boards with canonical printed cards; no pack admission. The focused probe was compiled and run on this baseline; it intentionally fails and is not green integration evidence.

## Authority and existing paths

`datasets/cards/cards.json` gives Stinger (12014) cost 1, Avenger ally, play only if the identity has Avenger, and excludes Stinger from the ally limit. Team-Building Exercise (12024) is a cost 2 support: Hero Action, exhaust it to play a hand card sharing a trait with the hero, reducing its resource cost by 1. Hank Pym (12011) is a cost 0 Avenger ally whose entry interrupt adds counters for resources overpaid, maximum four.

Current RR 1.8 `rr:ally-limit` permits play or put into play above the limit and requires the excess discard before entry abilities. `rr:ability.8.2` says a constant is active on entry and while in play. `rr:modifiers.3` makes a set modifier override non-set modifiers; `.4` floors cost at zero. Thus an extra limit point is not generally equivalent to excluding one ally from the count. `rr:resource.3,5` permit generating more than cost, with excess ordinarily lost. Historical official `ruling:8337b7a07cb58a64` (RR 1.5) specifically says Exercise does not reduce Hank's zero price or replace a resource overpaid for counters; the current clauses support that distinction and do not supersede it. `ruling:dc330186a6c8ca8e` and `ruling:e8c109f4af3b7aac` provide further Hank overpayment context, not an implementation of that separate prerequisite. The Magik as-if-in-hand ruling is a future origin interaction, not a reason to include ordinary discard cards in the hand selection.

`PrintedPlayRestrictions.IdentityTrait` parses Stinger's exact restriction and `PrintedCardPlayPrerequisites.Permits` reads the identity's current `Traits.Has`. The test checks Thor hero versus Odinson and restores the hero, with cost-1 Blade (21019) as the same-cost control. `CardEntry.CheckAllyLimit` sums every card in the controlled ally areas after placement, and `CardPlay.PutAllyIntoPlay` also enters that check. The actual-card theory compares Stinger and Blade after ordinary payment at three legal other allies. The compiled Stinger case failed at the expected limit-choice assertion: expected no choice, actual choice scheduled. The same-cost Blade case passed with a choice, and the identity restriction test passed.

For Exercise, `withTrait` accepts a fixed trait and queries current traits, but this alone does not compare a selected hand card's traits to a current hero's traits. Existing `makeTheCall` is an ally-from-discard procedure with printed cost and put-into-play, not a general nested card play. Ordinary `CardPayment.Price` excludes events, which use their action path; `CardPayment.CostOf` handles reductions and the zero floor. `ReduceNextMatchingCardCost` filters by a fixed kind/trait and a phase duration, not the selected card's live shared-hero-trait relation or a single nested play. A generic choose plus put-into-play would skip payment and event resolution. These observations bound candidate compositions, but do not prove every possible composition fails; no speculative DSL row is presented as an engine defect.
