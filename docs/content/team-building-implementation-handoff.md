## Exact reusable contracts and missing connection

* Candidate selection must enumerate the acting player's actual `Seat.Hand.Cards`, not `CardPlayOrigin.Candidates` alone (which may include other play permissions). Compare `Traits.Of(world, seat.IdentityCard, facts)` and `Traits.Of(world, card, facts)` live; `Traits.Of` accounts for granted/lost traits and active face. Preserve the Hero Action form gate through `AbilityOfferQueries.ActionAdmission` / `InForm`, and revalidate at the answer boundary.
* For ally/support/upgrade, `GameCardPlayOffers.Add` combines `CardPlayLegality.LegalAttachmentTargets` with `CardPayment.Price`; `CardPlayLegality.Permitted` includes location, printed restrictions, team-up, uniqueness and limits. `CardPayment.CostOf` computes ordinary modifiers and floor, `CardPlay.PlayAllocated` validates and calls `CardPlayPayment.Play`, whose `CardPlayInitiation` retains adjusted cost, targets and declared allocations. `CardPlayPayment.Prepare` performs payment preflight, and `CardPaymentEntry`/agenda handle suspended payment and entry. Reuse these behaviors with a scoped price input; preserve attachment-host-specific prices and resource requirements.
* Events use a different supported action path: `AbilityOfferQueries.Actions` finds authored event actions and gates `AbilityPaymentRules.EventPayable` / `ActionAdmission`; `AbilityPaymentPricing.CombinedPrice` / `EventPrice` and `AbilityEventPayment` separately call `CardPayment.CostOf`. Event selection must retain which eligible action and its targets/arrow cost; route through the ordinary event action lifecycle, including event initiation, cancellation, payment, and effect resolution. `CardPayment.Price` explicitly returns null for events, and `FreeCardPlay` and `PlayWithPermission` explicitly reject them. Identify the precise callable event invocation and its preflight/resumption while implementing; do not assume that raw `Actions` alone covers Exercise's effect-granted play timing. Probe supported action events first and explicitly reject unsupported event shapes rather than inventing legality.
* `AbilityResolutionChoices.Choosing` describes a persisted choice; `Chose` regenerates it and calls `DecisionAssessment.Assess` before tracking and applying. `AbilityStructuralExecution.DescribeMakeTheCall` / `AnswerMakeTheCall` illustrate priced affordances and live answer revalidation; `AbilityChoiceAnswer.ApplyMakeTheCall` and `AbilityResolutionEffectChoices.SuspendAfterProcedure` illustrate ownership of pending work and continuation. Reuse the choice and continuation pattern, not Make the Call semantics: it selects allies in discard and puts them into play, rather than playing an arbitrary hand card.
* `GameResolution.ValidateLiveCardPlay` demonstrates current-price and `CardPlayPayment.Prepare` checks before clearing information for root choices; it only handles the root card-play offer. The new nested choice needs equivalent live validation before any mutation in `AbilityResolutionChoices.Chose`, including exact card, form, traits, target, price and payment. Preserve the already-paid Exercise exhaust on rejection of a later answer, and preserve the pending prompt, state digest, RNG and agenda from that answer's precommit point.
