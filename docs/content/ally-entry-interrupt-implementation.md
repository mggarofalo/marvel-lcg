# Player ally entry interrupts

This engine prerequisite applies a player ally's play or put-into-play movement within the existing entry occurrence. `rr:interrupt.3` says an interrupt resolves before its condition resolves; `rr:initiating-abilities.1` permits an interrupt before the triggering condition becomes true. Only the exact pending physical ally's own entry Interrupt is admitted out of play. It remains optional. Played-card payment and entry-cost payment remain distinct receipts.

The operation captures the origin, incarnation and controller. Counter placements made by the pending ally's own interrupt are recorded for that occurrence and carried across the movement reset. The entry continuation captures the actual entering incarnation before responses; it cannot follow a card that leaves and reenters. The ally-limit continuation precedes entry responses. Reconciling the ally-limit text with pre-entry interrupts is a rules inference, supported by the interrupt clauses and the self-entry interrupt analogy in vendored `ruling:5be417ad9b193f46`. The rulebook does not explicitly order zero-health defeat against an immediately exceeded ally limit.

This prerequisite does not implement Hank Pym's excess-resource expression or zero-hit-point entry settlement, and does not add or admit his canonical ability row. The unresolved fourth-ally zero-health priority question remains outside this packet. A synthetic zero-health probe is preserved separately and is not included in this commit.
