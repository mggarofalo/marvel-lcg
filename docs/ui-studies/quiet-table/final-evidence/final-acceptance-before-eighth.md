## Review discipline

Reviewers receive game goals and scenarios, without source access or coached
click sequences. Before acting they explain cause, answering seat, optionality,
choices, costs, uncertainty and the next commitment; afterward they explain the
result. Native UI control is serialized. Actual render dimensions, scale,
motion, source identity and build identity accompany each batch. Confusion,
missing facts, failed interaction or concealed-information leakage is a finding.

Relevant fixes invalidate affected review evidence. A final commit must be
matched to the reviewed source; a rebase requires an integration assessment.
General choice fixtures unavailable in legal Core play remain explicitly
synthetic and do not establish expansion support. MARVEL-441 closes only when
its own criteria pass; ticket ownership cannot defer a MARVEL-443 requirement.
